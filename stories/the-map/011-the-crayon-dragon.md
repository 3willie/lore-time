# The Crayon Dragon

{{brother}} wanted the map to draw faster.

So one afternoon, he got his green crayon. And he drew on Grandpa's map.

A big, scribbly, wobbly dragon. Right in the middle.

{{hero}} came in. He stopped. His face went red.

"You drew on it! That's Grandpa's map! You've *ruined* it!"

{{brother}}'s eyes filled with tears. "I was helping."

{{hero}} stomped away. He sat on the stairs with his arms crossed. He was SO mad.

After a while, {{dad}} sat down next to him.

"He didn't mean to spoil it," said {{dad}}.

"I know," said {{hero}} in a small voice. "But it's special."

"It is. And so is he."

{{hero}} sat for a bit longer. The mad feeling got smaller. And smaller.

He went back in. {{brother}} was still sniffling.

"I'm sorry I shouted," said {{hero}}.

"I'm sorry I drew on it," said {{brother}}.

They looked at the scribbly green dragon.

"It's kind of a good dragon," said {{hero}}.

That night — *scritch, scratch* — the map began to draw.

Brown ink curled all around the crayon dragon. It gave him a ladder up his back. It gave him steps on his tail. It gave him a slide for a tongue.

Tiny letters appeared: *The Dragon Slide.*

The boys touched it together. *Swoosh!*

They were at the top of a huge green dragon, in a field of soft grass.

"After you," said {{hero}}.

"Together," said {{brother}}.

*WHEEEEE!*

Down the dragon's tongue they went.

It was the best place on the whole map.
