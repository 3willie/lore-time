# The Night the Lights Went Out

*Whoosh!* went the wind outside.

*Flicker, flicker…* went the lights.

And then — *click*. Everything went dark.

"The power's out," said {{dad}}. "It's the storm. It'll come back soon."

It was very, very dark. {{brother}} couldn't see his own hand.

"I don't like it," he whispered.

Then {{hero}} remembered. He dug in his pajama pocket.

"Think of Gull Rock," he said.

Two tiny lanterns began to glow. *Twinkle. Twinkle.* Just a little light. But in the dark, a little light is a lot.

"Ooh," said {{mom}}. "Those are handy."

{{dad}} found the flashlight. {{mom}} found the candles. Soon the living room was cozy and golden.

But {{hero}} was looking out the window.

Next door, at Mrs. Okafor's house, everything was dark. No candles. No flashlight.

"She's all by herself," said {{hero}}. "And her knees are sore. She might not find her way."

"Let's go see," said {{dad}}.

They put on raincoats. They carried the flashlight and the two little lanterns. *Splash, splash* across the yard.

*Knock, knock.*

"Oh, thank goodness," said Mrs. Okafor. "I couldn't find my candles. I was sitting in the dark."

The boys held up their lanterns, and she found her candles right away. Then {{mom}} brought over hot cocoa in a jug.

They all sat together, telling stories by candlelight.

When the lights came back on — *click!* — everybody said, "Awww."

"That was the best power outage ever," said {{brother}}.

"You were my lighthouse tonight," said Mrs. Okafor.
