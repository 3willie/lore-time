# The Map We Made

It was raining. *Drip, drip, drip.*

"I'm bored," said {{brother}}.

"Let's look at the map," said {{hero}}.

They unrolled Grandpa's map. They turned it over. Nothing new.

"It's not drawing anything today," sighed {{brother}}.

{{hero}} had an idea.

"What if *we* draw a map?"

{{brother}} sat up. "Of what?"

"Of our house! With secret places!"

They got a big piece of paper and all the crayons.

{{hero}} drew the kitchen. "Here's where the cookie jar lives."

{{brother}} drew the couch. "And here's the Cushion Mountains."

They drew the bathtub and called it the Bubble Sea. They drew the dark closet and called it the Cave of Coats. They drew the stairs and called them the Giant Steps.

"Now we need treasure," said {{hero}}.

They found {{dad}}'s favorite mug. They hid it somewhere very sneaky. And {{brother}} drew a big red X on the map.

When {{dad}} came home, they gave him the map.

"Your mug has been stolen by pirates," said {{brother}}. "Follow the map!"

{{dad}} looked at the map. He walked through the Cushion Mountains. He tiptoed past the Bubble Sea. He climbed the Giant Steps.

"X marks the spot!" said {{dad}}.

He opened the Cave of Coats. There was his mug, inside a boot.

"Treasure!" he cheered.

That night, the boys looked at Grandpa's map again.

In the middle of their little house, something new had appeared. A tiny drawing of a treasure map.

"I think Grandpa liked our map," said {{hero}}.
