# The New Map

In the wooden box in the attic, under Grandpa's letter, the boys found one more thing.

A roll of paper. Thick and creamy. And completely blank.

"Another map?" said {{brother}}.

There was a note pinned to it, in Grandpa's loopy writing.

*For the next explorer. You'll know who.*

"Who's the next explorer?" asked {{hero}}.

They thought and thought.

Mrs. Okafor already had a garden. Priya had the park.

Then {{mom}} called up the stairs. "Boys! Aunt Rosa is here with baby Theo!"

The boys looked at each other.

Baby Theo was very small. He couldn't walk. He couldn't talk. Mostly, he blew bubbles.

"He can't even hold a crayon," said {{brother}}.

"Not yet," said {{hero}}. "But he will."

They carried the map downstairs. They unrolled it on the rug, next to baby Theo.

"This is for you," said {{hero}}. "When you're bigger."

"When you do something kind," said {{brother}}, "it draws a new place. I'll show you how."

Baby Theo looked at the paper. He looked at the boys. Then he reached out and gave {{brother}} his soggy rattle.

"He shared!" said {{brother}}.

Everybody laughed.

And in the very middle of the new map, a tiny drop of brown ink appeared. It curled into a little house.

Baby Theo's house.

"It's started," whispered {{hero}}.

"I think Grandpa would like that," said {{dad}} softly.

Upstairs, on the old map, a new path was drawing itself too.

There is always somewhere new to go.
