# First Flight

Today was the first flight!

The saddles were soft, with straps to keep you safe. "Hold on tight," said Master Oona.

{{brother}} and Cinder went first.

Cinder was SO excited. He wiggled. He hopped. He flapped so hard he took off before {{brother}} was ready!

"Easy, Cinder," said {{brother}}, patting his neck. "Together."

Cinder took a deep breath. He ran — *flap, flap, flap* — and lifted up into the air!

It was bumpy. It was wobbly. But they were flying! One slow circle around the yard… and then *flump*, they landed in a big pile of hay.

{{brother}} popped up with hay in his hair. "WE FLEW!"

Then it was {{hero}}'s turn.

Skye didn't run. She *jumped*. Two big flaps of her wings, and they zoomed straight up — past the castle, into the clouds!

It was amazing! The wind! The sky! The mountains far below!

"Faster!" shouted {{hero}}. "Go faster! Turn! Go down! Go up!"

He pulled the reins this way and that way. Bossing, bossing, bossing.

Skye stopped in the air. Then she tipped sideways — very gently — and dropped him into the lake.

*SPLASH!*

{{hero}} came up spluttering. Skye flew in circles above him. She didn't look angry. She looked like she was waiting.

Master Oona helped him out of the water and gave him a towel.

"She dropped me!" said {{hero}}.

"She did," said Master Oona. "Gently. Into water. On purpose. You weren't flying *with* her. You were bossing her. Would *you* like to be bossed?"

{{hero}} thought about it. "No," he said.

"A good rider doesn't boss," said Master Oona. "A good rider *asks*."

Skye landed beside him and nudged his wet hair.

"Sorry, Skye," said {{hero}}. "Can we try again? I'll ask nicely this time."

Skye bent down low, so he could climb on.
