# What They Built, Part Two

Arbok and Weezing came first — the coordinated pair, Arbok cutting low while Weezing floated wide to flank.

"Arcanine — Arbok. Dragonair — Weezing."

Arcanine intercepted Arbok with Extreme Speed, the collision stopping Arbok's momentum entirely. Close quarters, relentless, Arcanine fighting with the controlled aggression of a Pokémon that understood exactly how serious this was.

Dragonair swept wide and hit Weezing with Thunder Wave before it could deploy its Smokescreen — grounding it, slowing it, following immediately with Dragon Rage that sent Weezing spinning into the compound wall.

Electrode rolled fast from the right — Koda felt the electrical charge building.

*It's going to Explode.*

"Dragonair — Wrap it. Hold it away from the cages!"

Dragonair moved faster than Koda had seen it move — crossing the compound in a fluid surge, coiling around Electrode before it could reach the cage line. The explosion risk contained. Dragonair held it away from everything that mattered, the electrical discharge crawling painfully across its scales.

"Hold!" Koda called.

Dragonair held.

Golbat came screaming from above — diving fast, Supersonic aimed at Arcanine still locked with Arbok.

Koda stepped forward.

He let the air move.

Not subtle. Not careful. A hard upward column that hit Golbat mid-dive and redirected it straight up — fast and disorienting, sending it tumbling above the compound into open sky where it couldn't target anyone.

An agent shouted. Pointed at Koda.

Three agents converged on him directly.

Victreebel launched from the left — all acid and vine, fast and aggressive.

Koda felt everything at once — Dragonair straining with Electrode, Arcanine still fighting Arbok, Golbat recovering above, Victreebel closing fast, three agents between him and any exit.

*Okay.*
