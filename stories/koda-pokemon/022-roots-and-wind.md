# Roots and Wind

The valley opened into a stretch of tall grass just outside Vermilion City's outskirts. Koda could see the city's rooftops in the distance, the faint smell of ocean air drifting in from the south.

He was watching Pidgeot glide lazy circles overhead when the ground shifted.

Not an earthquake. Something smaller. Deliberate.

A thick root curled up from the soil near the tree line, tapped the path twice, then retreated.

Koda stopped walking. A slow smile crossed his face.

Only one person he knew did that.

She stepped out from the tree line, arms crossed, a quiet grin matching his.

"Meadow."

"Took you long enough to feel that," she said. "I've been watching you walk for five minutes."

---

She looked the same — steady, unhurried, like she was always exactly where she meant to be. Two Poké Balls on her belt. A third clipped to her jacket.

"You're here too," Koda said.

"Apparently we all ended up somewhere." She glanced up at Pidgeot still circling. "You caught a Pidgeot."

"Last week. You?"

She held up two fingers. "Bulbasaur. Geodude." A pause. "They suit me."

Koda laughed quietly. Of course they did.

"Two on two?" he asked.

She was already reaching for her belt. "I was going to suggest it myself."

---

## Kadabra & Pidgeot vs. Bulbasaur & Geodude

Meadow opened calmly, Bulbasaur and Geodude side by side. Koda sent out Kadabra and Pidgeot.

Immediately the ground churned — Meadow's presence was subtle but unmistakable to Koda. Geodude sank slightly lower, grip on the earth tightening. Bulbasaur's vines moved with just a little more purpose than normal.

*She's grounding them. Steadying their footing.*

Geodude launched Rollout toward Kadabra. Bulbasaur sent twin vines sweeping low at Pidgeot's landing space, cutting off easy repositioning.

"Pidgeot — stay high. Kadabra — Teleport, then Psybeam on Geodude."

Kadabra vanished and reappeared cleanly behind Geodude, landing a sharp Psybeam. Geodude tumbled — but the earth seemed to slow its roll, letting it recover faster than it should have.

*She felt that coming.*

Bulbasaur launched Razor Leaf at Kadabra while it was repositioning. Several leaves connected. Kadabra winced.

"Pidgeot — Steel Wing on Geodude, dive fast!"

Pidgeot folded and came down hard. The hit cracked across Geodude's surface. This time it didn't recover.

One down.

But Bulbasaur had used the distraction well — vines wrapped around Kadabra's legs, holding it in place. Sleep Powder followed immediately.

Kadabra was down before it could teleport free.

Koda took a steady breath. *Fair.*

Pidgeot alone against Bulbasaur.

"Pidgeot — Gust, wide and low. Push the vines back."

Pidgeot beat its wings hard, sending a ground-level gust that ripped the extended vines sideways. Bulbasaur lost its footing for just a moment — enough. A clean Wing Attack landed center mass.

Bulbasaur sat down and didn't get up.

---

Meadow recalled her Pokémon quietly, same way Koda did — with a pause, a moment of acknowledgment.

"Your Pidgeot is fast," she said.

"Your Bulbasaur almost won it alone," he replied.

She almost smiled. "Almost."

---

They sat at the tree line for a while after, Arcanine lying in the grass nearby, Bulbasaur resting beside Meadow with quiet ease. Pidgeot perched in the branches above.

"Do you think others from home ended up here too?" Meadow asked.

Koda looked out toward Vermilion City.

"I think we'll find out."

She nodded slowly, fingers pressing lightly into the soil beside her — just resting there, connected.

The wind moved through the grass between them.

Neither needed to say anything else.
