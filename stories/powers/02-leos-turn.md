# Leo's Turn

Leo tried everything to find his power.

He pointed at the tap like Max did. No spark. He jumped off the bottom stair to see if he could fly. He landed on his bottom. He stared at a spoon for a whole minute to see if he could bend it. The spoon stayed exactly spoon-shaped.

"It's not fair," Leo said, flopping onto the sofa. "Max got a power and I got *nothing*."

Max sat down next to him. His fingertip glowed blue for a moment, then faded. "Maybe yours is just shy."

That afternoon, the family went to the park. A little girl was crying by the swings — she had lost her teddy. Her mum was searching under benches, looking worried.

Leo walked over and knelt down. He didn't know why, but he closed his eyes and listened. Under the wind, under the birds, under the shouting of kids on the slide, he heard something very, very quiet.

A rustle. Deep in the hedge.

He crawled in and pulled out a slightly muddy teddy bear.

The little girl stopped crying at once. "Thank you!"

On the walk home, Max looked at his brother. "How did you know where it was?"

"I just… heard it," Leo said. "Really, *really* well."

Dad raised his eyebrows. "Super hearing. That's a power for helping people. You noticed someone who needed you."

Leo grinned all the way home. His power wasn't flashy. It didn't glow. But it was exactly right for him.

That night, lying in bed, Leo listened. Far away, past the garden and the street, something was making a strange humming sound…
