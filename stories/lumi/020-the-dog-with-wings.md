# The Dog with Wings

{{brother}} ran into the kitchen, holding {{dad}}'s phone.

"LOOK!" he shouted. "A dog with WINGS! A real flying dog!"

On the screen was a picture of a fluffy white dog. It had big feathery wings. It was flying over a field.

"Lumi! Is it real?" asked {{brother}}.

*Blink, blink.*

"What do you think?" said Lumi. "Have you ever seen a dog with wings?"

{{brother}} thought. "No. But it's in a picture. So it must be real."

"Here's something important," said Lumi. "Some pictures are made up. A person can draw them. Or a computer helper, like me, can make them."

"*You* can make pictures?" asked {{hero}}.

"Some helpers can," said Lumi. "They can make a dog with wings. Or a cat on the moon. Or a whale in a bathtub. They look real. But they're pretend."

{{brother}} looked at the dog again. "So it's not real?"

"How could we check?" asked Lumi.

{{hero}} thought. "We could ask {{dad}}."

{{dad}} looked. "That's made up," he said. "A fun picture. But dogs don't have wings."

"Aww," said {{brother}}.

"Making pretend pictures can be fun," said Lumi. "Like when you draw. The trick is knowing what's pretend and what's real."

"How do we know?" asked {{brother}}.

"Ask yourself: *does this happen in real life?*" said Lumi. "And if you're not sure, ask a grown-up."

{{brother}} got his crayons. He drew a dog. Then he gave it wings. Big, purple, sparkly ones.

"This is pretend," he said proudly. "I know because I made it."

"That's the best kind," said Lumi.
