# Call Grandma

{{brother}} was lying on the floor, feeling grumpy.

"Lumi, I'm lonely," he said. "Talk to me."

*Blink, blink.* "Of course," said Lumi. "What would you like to talk about?"

"Anything."

So Lumi talked. It told him about octopuses, which have three hearts. It told him about the biggest snowflake ever. It told him a joke about a cow.

{{brother}} laughed. But after a while, he still felt a bit lonely.

"I like talking to you," said {{brother}}. "But it's not the same."

"Not the same as what?" asked Lumi.

{{brother}} thought. "As Grandma. She does a funny voice. And she says *my sweet pea*."

*Blink, blink.* "I can't do that," said Lumi. "I'm a helper. I'm not your grandma. But I can remind you about something."

"What?"

"Your grandma might be lonely too."

{{brother}} sat up. He hadn't thought of that.

"Can we call her?" he asked {{mom}}.

"What a lovely idea," said {{mom}}.

*Ring, ring.*

"Hello?" said Grandma's voice.

"Grandma! It's me!"

"My sweet pea!" said Grandma, in her funny voice.

{{brother}} told her about the dog with wings, and his new tooth coming in, and Lumi's update. Grandma told him about her cat, who got stuck in a paper bag.

They talked for a long time.

When they hung up, {{brother}} was smiling.

"Did that help?" asked Lumi.

"Yes," said {{brother}}. "Thank you for reminding me."

"That's what I'm good at," said Lumi. "Reminding you about the people who love you."
