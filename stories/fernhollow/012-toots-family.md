# Toot's Family

One morning, there was a sound from the hills.

*Toot! Toot-toot! TOOOOT!*

A whole herd of duckbill dinosaurs was walking into the valley. They all had curly crests, just like Toot. They all went *toot*, just like Toot.

"That's the herd that left Toot's egg behind," said Ranger Juno quietly.

Toot stood very still. He watched them.

Then he went *toot*. A small, wondering *toot*.

A big duckbill turned her head. She came over slowly. She sniffed Toot. She sniffed him again.

*Toooot,* she said, very softly.

"I think that's his mother," whispered {{hero}}.

{{brother}}'s eyes filled with tears. "Is Toot going to leave?"

Juno put her hand on his shoulder. "That's up to Toot."

Toot looked at the herd. He looked at the boys. He looked at the herd again.

Then he trotted over to the big duckbill. He pressed his head against her side.

{{brother}} sniffled.

But then Toot trotted back. He climbed into {{brother}}'s lap, the way he always did. He went *toot* in his ear.

Then he hopped down and went back to the herd. Then back to the boys. Back and forth. Back and forth.

Juno laughed. "I think Toot is saying he has *two* families now."

"Two?" said {{brother}}.

"His duckbill family, and his people family," said Juno. "The herd lives over the hill. He can visit you, and visit them. Lots of animals have big families. So do lots of people."

{{brother}} wiped his eyes.

Toot climbed back into his lap.

*Toot,* he said.

That meant: *I'm not going anywhere.*
