# The Sky Trial

Today was the Sky Trial! A race through the Needle Rocks — tall, pointy rocks poking out of the clouds.

The winner would get a golden ribbon.

"Skye's going to win," said {{brother}}. "She's the fastest."

But {{brother}} looked a little worried. Cinder was the smallest, slowest dragon of all.

*TOOOOT!* went the horn. Off they flew!

Skye was amazing. {{hero}} didn't boss. He asked. And Skye zoomed between the rocks like a fish swimming through reeds. Soon they were way out in front. The finish line was right there!

Then {{hero}} heard something. Far behind him, in the clouds.

*Hic!* A scared little hiccup.

Cinder!

{{hero}} looked at the finish line. The golden ribbon was *so* close.

Then he looked back at the clouds.

"Skye," he said. "Can we go back for my brother?"

Skye turned right around.

In the cloud, it was gray and foggy. And there was Cinder, holding on to a rock, too scared to move. {{brother}} was holding on tight.

"We got lost," said {{brother}} in a wobbly voice. "Cinder can't see the way."

"Follow us," said {{hero}}. "Stay right by Skye's tail."

So Cinder let go of the rock. And slowly, side by side, the two dragons flew out of the cloud together.

Cinder was so happy that he hiccupped. *Hic!*

But this time, it wasn't smoke. It was a tiny orange *flame*!

"Cinder!" shouted {{brother}}. "You made FIRE!"

Cinder looked very proud.

They crossed the finish line last. Both of them.

A girl named Brenna won the golden ribbon. Everyone clapped for her.

Afterward, Master Oona came to see the boys.

"Anyone can win a race," she said. "But the *real* trial was who would turn back to help." She smiled. "You both passed. Next week, we fly to the Ember Isles, where the baby wild dragons live. Get ready!"
