# The Vermilion Challenge, Part One

## The S.S. Anne & Lt. Surge's Test

Vermilion City smelled like the ocean and engine oil. The harbor dominated the southern edge of town — massive cargo ships, the famous S.S. Anne docked at the far pier, trainers moving in every direction.

Koda had heard about Lt. Surge before arriving. Everyone had.

*The Lightning American. Vermilion's Gym Leader. Former military. Doesn't lose.*

He spent his first day in the city the way he always did — watching, listening, learning the terrain. He walked the docks. Talked to other trainers. Pieced together what he could.

What he learned wasn't encouraging.

Surge's Pokémon hit hard and fast. His signature was Raichu — evolved, powerful, built for speed and overwhelming electrical output. He didn't give opponents time to think.

*Fast and electric,* Koda thought. *Water nearby. Enclosed gym space.*

He filed every detail.

---

That evening, the S.S. Anne was open to trainers. Koda boarded with his team and spent three hours in back-to-back battles below deck — narrow corridors, unpredictable footing, close quarters.

Exactly the wrong conditions for big wind moves. Exactly why he chose it.

*If they can battle well in here, they can battle anywhere.*

Kadabra thrived in the tight space — precision over power, Teleport limited to short bursts, Psybeam work sharp and efficient.

Pidgeot struggled at first. The ceiling was too low for proper flight. But by the fourth battle it had adapted — using short wing bursts for speed rather than altitude, learning to be dangerous without open sky.

Arcanine was steady throughout. Controlled bursts. Reading angles. Never once needing more space than it had.

By the time they left the ship, all three were tired in the right way.

---

The gym doors were steel. Heavy. The kind that said *this is serious* before you ever stepped inside.

Koda pushed them open.

The interior was enormous — polished floors, high ceilings, industrial lighting that hummed faintly. Trainers stationed throughout, each blocking the path forward. Koda would have to work through all of them to reach Surge.

He took a breath.

*One at a time.*

The first trainer sent out Voltorb. Koda nodded to Arcanine.

*Your battle. Show me what you've learned.*

Arcanine read the field without instruction — circling wide to avoid the electrical discharge radius, wearing Voltorb down with measured Ember bursts until it couldn't continue.

No commands needed.

Koda felt something settle in his chest. *There it is.*

Trainer after trainer fell. Kadabra handled the Pikachu pairs — calm, methodical, never flashy. Pidgeot dismantled a Fearow that thought it had the air advantage and discovered quickly it didn't.

By the time Koda reached the far end of the gym, his team was warmed up, focused, and ready.

Lt. Surge was already watching from across the floor. Arms crossed. A full head taller than Koda expected.

He looked down at him for a long moment.

"You're the kid making noise," he said. His voice was like gravel in a metal drum. "Boulder Badge. Cascade Badge." He glanced at the team. "Kadabra. Arcanine. Pidgeot." A pause. "You trained on the S.S. Anne last night."

Koda didn't ask how he knew.

"Three on three?" Koda asked.

Surge almost smiled.

"Three on three."

---

He opened with Electrode.

Fast. Faster than Voltorb had any right to evolve into. It launched a Thunder Wave before Koda had finished sending out Kadabra — and it connected, static locking Kadabra's legs mid-step.

*Paralysis. It's going to be slower now.*

"Kadabra — Psybeam. Don't rush it."

Kadabra fired through the static. Electrode dodged — but barely. It came back with Thunderbolt, and this time it hit clean.

Kadabra went down.

Koda recalled it steadily. No frustration. *That was Electrode's job. It did it well.*

"Pidgeot."

Electrode targeted Pidgeot immediately with another Thunder Wave — but Pidgeot was already climbing, fast, using the gym's high ceiling to its full advantage for the first time.

*This is why we trained on the ship. So you'd appreciate open space when you had it.*

"Steel Wing — diagonal dive, pull up sharp!"

Pidgeot folded and dropped like a blade, connecting hard before Electrode could track the angle change. The impact sent Electrode skidding across the polished floor.

It didn't get back up.

Surge watched without expression. Reached for his second ball.

Raichu stepped onto the floor.

The air in the gym changed. Even the industrial lights seemed to flicker once.

Koda looked at Pidgeot — tired but standing — then at Arcanine waiting calmly at his side.

*Here we go.*
