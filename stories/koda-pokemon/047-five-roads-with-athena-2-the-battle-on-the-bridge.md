# Five Roads With Athena 2: The Battle on the Bridge

A wide stone bridge crossed a slow river two hours north of the village. A trainer was already on it — older, maybe fourteen, blocking the center with the specific energy of someone who had been waiting to battle someone worth battling.

He had three Pokémon on his belt and a practiced stance.

"Two on two," he said. "I'll take you both at once."

Athena looked at Koda. Koda looked at Athena.

"Together?" she said.

"Together."

---

The trainer opened with Kadabra — not Koda's Kadabra, his own — and a Growlithe. The double type spread was smart. Balanced.

Koda sent Pidgeot. Athena sent Eevee.

Pidgeot and the opposing Kadabra immediately became a speed contest — Teleport versus aerial repositioning, neither able to land clean. The bridge's stone walls limited Pidgeot's angles, forcing tighter turns than it preferred.

*Work with what you have,* Koda thought. *Not what you want.*

"Pidgeot — use the walls. Bank off the left side."

Pidgeot read it immediately, using the stone wall as a redirect point, Steel Wing catching the opposing Kadabra mid-Teleport reappearance.

Kadabra went down.

Eevee and Growlithe had been a different kind of battle entirely — close, scrappy, neither backing down. Eevee was smaller but faster, and Athena gave instructions in a rhythm that was almost conversational rather than commanding.

"Left, Eevee. Wait. Now — Swift."

Eevee released a spray of stars that Growlithe couldn't dodge at close range.

Growlithe sat down.

The trainer stared at Eevee.

"Your Eevee listens like it can actually understand you."

Athena scratched behind Eevee's ears. "It can."

She said it so naturally the trainer didn't know how to respond.

They crossed the bridge and kept walking.
