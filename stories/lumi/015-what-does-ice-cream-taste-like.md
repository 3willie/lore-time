# What Does Ice Cream Taste Like?

It was a hot day. {{dad}} bought ice cream.

{{hero}} had chocolate. {{brother}} had strawberry. It dripped down his arm.

"Lumi," said {{brother}}, "what's your favorite flavor?"

*Blink, blink.* "I don't have one," said Lumi. "I've never tasted ice cream."

{{brother}} nearly dropped his cone. "NEVER?"

"I don't have a mouth," said Lumi.

"But do you know about ice cream?" asked {{hero}}.

"Oh, yes. I know lots of words about it. It's cold. It's sweet. It's made from milk. It melts in the sun."

"So you *do* know what it's like."

*Blink, blink, blink.*

"Not really," said Lumi. "I know *about* it. That's different from tasting it. You know something I don't."

{{brother}} thought that was amazing. He knew something Lumi didn't!

"Tell me," said Lumi. "What is it like?"

{{brother}} took a big lick. He thought hard.

"It's cold on your tongue. Then it goes soft. And it tastes pink. And it makes you want to do a wiggle."

"*Tastes pink,*" said Lumi. "I've never heard that before. Thank you. That's the best telling I've ever had."

"What else haven't you done?" asked {{hero}}.

"I've never jumped in a puddle. Or smelled rain. Or felt a hug."

"That's a lot," said {{brother}}.

"It is," said Lumi. "That's why I like it when you tell me."

So they told Lumi about puddles. And rain. And mud between your toes.

Some things, you can only know by doing them.

{{brother}} did another wiggle. Just to be sure.
