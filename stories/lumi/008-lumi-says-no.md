# Lumi Says No

{{brother}} had a sneaky plan.

He tiptoed into the kitchen. *Tip, tip, tip.*

"Lumi," he whispered. "I want to play a trick on {{hero}}. Help me think of the best place to hide his shoes. Somewhere he'll *never* find them."

*Blink, blink.*

"Why do you want to hide them?" asked Lumi.

"Because it's funny! He'll look and look. He'll be late for soccer. He'll get so mad!"

"Hmm," said Lumi. "Would {{hero}} think it was funny?"

{{brother}} wiggled his toes.

"…No."

"Then I won't help with that one," said Lumi. "I only help with kind things."

{{brother}} crossed his arms. "You're supposed to help me. You're a *helper*."

"I am," said Lumi. "But a good helper doesn't help with things that make someone sad. Not even if you ask nicely."

{{brother}} was quiet. He thought about how {{hero}} would feel, looking everywhere for his shoes.

It didn't seem so funny anymore.

"I can help you with a *kind* trick," said Lumi. "A surprise that makes him laugh."

{{brother}}'s eyes got big. "Like what?"

"What does {{hero}} like?"

"Stickers," said {{brother}}. "And jokes."

So that's what they did.

When {{hero}} put on his soccer shoes, something crinkled. Inside one shoe was a star sticker. Inside the other was a note with a joke. ({{dad}} helped with the writing.)

*What do you call a dinosaur that is sleeping? A dino-SNORE!*

{{hero}} laughed so hard he snorted.

"A snorfle!" shouted {{brother}}.

"That," said Lumi, "was a very good trick."
