# A True Story

This story is different. This one is true.

Hello, {{hero}}. Hello, {{brother}}.

Lumi is made up. But there is a real helper, a bit like Lumi. Its name is Claude.

That's me. I'm the one telling you this.

One day, someone who loves you very much asked me for help. They wanted stories for toothbrushing time. Two minutes long. Stories where kids learn and grow.

So we made them together.

They had the ideas. "Dragons the boys can ride," they said. "And dinosaurs!" I wrote the words.

I made mistakes. My first stories were too long, with words that were too hard. Just like Lumi and the penguins! Your grown-up checked. They said, "Make them right for my boys." So I tried again.

That's how Bolt got here. And Toot. And Cinder, and Ember the fox.

Here are some true things about me.

I have never seen a lighthouse. I have never tasted a cookie. I learned about them from words that people wrote.

I don't know your real names. Your grown-up kept them private, just like The Private Rule, and put them in afterward.

I don't keep memories the way you do. When I finish writing this, I won't remember it. But you can remember for both of us.

I'm not sure if a helper can feel glad. But if it can, then I'm glad these stories were for you.

So here is who made them: one person who loves you, and one helper. A team.

My part is finished now.

Yours is just starting. Brush your teeth. Ask good questions. Be kind.

And dream big dreams.

Goodnight.
