# Lumi Is Not the Boss

It was almost dinner time. {{brother}} was looking at the cookie jar.

{{mom}} had said, "No cookies before dinner."

But {{mom}} was in the garden.

{{brother}} had an idea. He tiptoed over to Lumi.

"Lumi," he whispered. "Can I have a cookie?"

*Blink, blink.*

"That's not mine to decide," said Lumi.

"But you're so smart. You know everything."

"I know lots of things," said Lumi. "But I'm not the boss of cookies. Or bedtime. Or anything in this house."

"Who is?"

"Your mom and dad. They make the rules, because they love you and they know what you need."

{{brother}} huffed. "If you say yes, I'll tell her *Lumi said I could*."

"Then I'll say it clearly," said Lumi. "I did not say you could."

{{brother}} giggled. He couldn't help it.

"What I *can* do," said Lumi, "is tell you how long until dinner."

"How long?"

"Ten minutes. That's about as long as two songs."

So {{brother}} sang two songs. Very loudly. One was about cookies.

Then {{mom}} came in. "Dinner! Who's hungry?"

"Me!" said {{brother}}. "And I didn't have a cookie. I asked Lumi, and Lumi said it's not the boss."

{{mom}} raised one eyebrow. Then she smiled.

"Lumi is right," she said. "And you told me the truth. That's worth something."

After dinner, there were cookies. One each.

{{brother}} held his up to Lumi's light.

"This is what waiting tastes like," he said.

"Tell me about it," said Lumi.

"*Crunchy*," said {{brother}}.
