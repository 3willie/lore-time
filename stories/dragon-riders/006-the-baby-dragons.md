# The Baby Dragons

The Ember Isles were warm. Steam puffed out of the ground. *Pfff!*

"The baby wild dragons live here," whispered Master Oona. "They've never seen people. Be gentle."

{{brother}} couldn't wait. He ran up the hill.

"BABY DRAGONS! WHERE ARE YOU?"

*Zip! Zoom! Scatter!*

Tiny tails vanished behind the rocks. Then it was very quiet.

"Oops," said {{brother}}.

{{hero}} remembered how he had made friends with Skye.

"Don't chase," he said. "Sit down. Let them come."

So the boys sat down on the warm ground. They kept still. Cinder and Skye lay down too.

One minute went by.

A small blue nose peeked over a rock. Then two yellow eyes.

"Don't move," whispered {{hero}}.

A baby dragon crept out. She was no bigger than a kitten. She sniffed {{brother}}'s shoe. She sniffed his knee.

Then — *snatch!* — she grabbed his hat and ran!

"Hey!" giggled {{brother}}.

The baby dragon put the hat on her own head. It covered her whole face. She bumped into a rock. *Bonk.*

The boys laughed. Cinder went *hic!*

Out came more babies. A red one. A green one. Two purple twins. They climbed on Cinder. They slid down Skye's tail. They all wanted a turn with the hat.

Soon {{brother}} had one baby dragon on each knee.

"You found the secret," said Master Oona. "Loud scares them off. Quiet brings them close."

The little blue one curled up in {{brother}}'s lap. She was still wearing his hat.

"You can keep it," he whispered.
