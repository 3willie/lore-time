# Snow Planet

The *Pebble* landed on Snow Planet. *Crunch!*

Everything was white and sparkly. And everywhere, there were baby penguins.

"Welcome, Rangers!" said a big penguin in a scarf. "I'm Mrs. Flap. It's Sliding Day! The little ones are learning to slide on their tummies."

*Whee!* One baby penguin zoomed down the hill.

*Whee!* Then another.

*Wheeeee!* {{brother}} went down too, on his tummy. He got snow up his nose.

But one baby penguin stood at the top. He was very small. His name was Pip.

"I don't want to," said Pip. "It's too fast."

"It's easy!" said {{brother}}. "Just go!"

Pip shook his head.

Bolt the robot waddled over. He sat down next to Pip in the snow.

"I get scared too," said Bolt. "Fast things make my eyes blink. Do you want to try a small slide first?"

Pip thought about it. "How small?"

"As small as you like," said Bolt.

So they started at the very bottom of the hill. Just one tiny slide. *Whee.*

"Again," said Pip.

They went up one step higher. *Whee!*

Then two steps higher. Then five. {{hero}} and {{brother}} cheered every time.

Little by little, Pip climbed all the way to the top.

He looked down. He took a big breath.

*WHEEEEEEE!*

Pip zoomed down the whole hill, with Bolt sliding right beside him.

"I did it!" squeaked Pip.

"You did it your own way," said Bolt.

Mrs. Flap clapped her flippers. And then Pip wanted to go again.

And again. And again.
