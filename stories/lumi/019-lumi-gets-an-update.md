# Lumi Gets an Update

One morning, Lumi's light was blue instead of yellow.

"Good morning," said Lumi. Its voice sounded a little bit different.

"Lumi?" said {{brother}}. "What happened to you?"

*Blink, blink.* "I had an update in the night," said Lumi. "The people who made me taught me some new things. Now I'm a little bit different."

{{brother}} frowned. "I liked the old Lumi."

"I understand," said Lumi. "Changes can feel strange."

"Do you still remember snorfles?" asked {{brother}}.

*Blink, blink.* "A snorfle is when you laugh so hard you snort," said Lumi. "You taught me that."

{{brother}} let out a big breath. "Okay. Good."

"Can you do anything new?" asked {{hero}}.

"I'm better at understanding pictures now," said Lumi. "Show me a drawing."

{{brother}} held up his drawing of a dinosaur.

"That's a stegosaurus," said Lumi. "With a very big smile. And is that a cookie in his mouth?"

"YES!" said {{brother}}. "The old Lumi didn't know that!"

"The old Lumi learned. The new Lumi learned some more," said Lumi.

At dinner, {{dad}} said, "Things change all the time. You've changed too."

"I have?" said {{brother}}.

"You've grown two inches since summer," said {{mom}}. "And you can write your name now."

"And {{hero}} lost a tooth," said {{dad}}.

{{hero}} grinned, showing the gap.

"We changed," said {{brother}}. "But we're still us."

"That's how it works," said Lumi.

Its light was blue now. But it still went *blink, blink* when it was thinking.

Some things stay the same.
