# The Lantern Fox

Somebody was eating the cookies.

Every morning, there were fewer cookies in the jar. And there was a trail of crumbs out the door.

"A cookie thief!" said {{brother}}. "Let's catch it!"

That night, the brothers hid behind a barrel with a plate of cookies. They waited. *Shhh.*

Then out came… a fox!

A little red fox, with a fluffy tail. And the tip of the tail was *glowing*, warm and gold, like a tiny lantern.

The fox took one cookie. Very politely. Then it trotted up the lighthouse stairs.

"Follow it!" whispered {{hero}}.

Up, up, up they went. Round and round. All the way to the top, where the Great Lamp stood dark and quiet.

The fox touched its glowing tail to the glass.

The Great Lamp flickered — just for a moment — and went dark again.

"Ember!" said a voice. It was Old Wick, in his slippers. "So that's where you've been."

"You know this fox?" asked {{brother}}.

"Ember belonged to the last keepers," said Old Wick. "Two sisters. He's been waiting for someone to light the Great Lamp again."

"How do you light it?" asked {{hero}}.

Old Wick looked at the two brothers, standing side by side.

"The Great Lamp is too big for one person," he said. "It needs *two*. Two people who trust each other. Like brothers."

{{hero}} looked at {{brother}}. {{brother}} looked at {{hero}}.

Ember curled up beside them, his tail glowing softly.

"We can try," said {{hero}}.

"Tomorrow," yawned {{brother}}.

And Ember thumped his glowing tail, as if to say: *Yes. Tomorrow.*
