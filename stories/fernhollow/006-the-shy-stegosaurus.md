# The Shy Stegosaurus

On feast day, the clearing was full of dinosaurs.

Triceratops brought leaves. Tallulah the long-neck brought treetop fruit. The little Zippers brought shiny pebbles, which nobody could eat, but everybody said thank you.

"Where's Spike?" asked Ranger Juno.

Spike was a stegosaurus. He had big plates on his back and a long, spiky tail.

The boys found him hiding behind a tree. Well, *trying* to hide. He was much bigger than the tree.

"Aren't you coming?" asked {{brother}}.

Spike shook his head. He swished his tail sadly.

*CRASH!* The tail knocked over a log.

Spike hung his head.

"Oh," said {{hero}}. "Is that why? Your tail bumps into things?"

Spike nodded. Last year, he had knocked over the whole fruit pile. Everybody had stared.

"You can't help your tail," said {{brother}}. "It's just your shape."

{{hero}} had an idea. "What if we make a Spike-sized space?"

They ran back to the clearing. They rolled the logs out of the way. They moved the fruit pile to the other side. They made a big, wide, empty spot, with soft ferns to sit on.

{{brother}} put flowers all around it, so everyone would know it was special.

"Spike!" they called. "Your place is ready!"

Spike peeked out. He walked over slowly. He sat down in his spot. He swished his tail.

It didn't hit anything at all.

Spike made a happy, rumbly sound. Tallulah passed him a treetop fruit.

"There's room for everybody," said Juno. "Sometimes you just have to move the logs."
