# When I Grow Up

The stars were out. {{hero}} and {{brother}} sat on the back steps with {{dad}}. Lumi glowed beside them.

"Lumi," said {{hero}}, "will there be helpers like you when I'm grown up?"

*Blink, blink.* "I think there will be lots," said Lumi. "Even better ones than me."

"What will they do?"

"That depends on you," said Lumi. "What do you want to do when you grow up?"

{{hero}} thought. "I want to be an animal doctor."

"Then a helper could listen to a puppy's heartbeat with you," said Lumi. "And help you find out why a kitten feels sick. But *you* would be the one to hold its paw."

{{hero}} smiled.

"I want to go to the bottom of the ocean!" said {{brother}}. "And find a fish nobody has ever seen."

"Then a helper could steer your submarine through the dark," said Lumi. "And help you draw a map. But *you* would be the one to say, *Look! Over there!*"

{{brother}} bounced on the step.

"What about you, {{dad}}?" asked {{hero}}. "Did you have helpers when you were little?"

{{dad}} laughed. "No. We only dreamed about them. You two are the first kids to grow up with them."

Everyone was quiet, looking at the stars.

"Lumi," said {{hero}}, "what's your job, when we're grown up?"

"The same as now," said Lumi. "To help. To be honest. And to say *I'm not sure* when I'm not sure."

"And what's our job?" asked {{brother}}.

"The dreaming," said Lumi. "The trying. The caring. Those were always yours."

{{dad}} put an arm around each boy.

Way up high, a tiny star went *blink, blink*.

Just like Lumi.
