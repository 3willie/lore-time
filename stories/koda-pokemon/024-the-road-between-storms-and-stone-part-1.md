# The Road Between Storms and Stone, Part One

They didn't make a formal decision. Meadow simply fell into step beside him as he stood to leave, and Koda didn't question it. Some things didn't need to be said out loud.

Arcanine walked between them. Bulbasaur rode on Meadow's shoulder like it had always been there.

---

## Battle One — The Riverside Twins

Two siblings — a girl and a boy, maybe nine and eleven — were training near a shallow river crossing when they spotted Koda and Meadow.

"Double battle!" the older one called out. "We've been practicing!"

Their Pokémon were Psyduck and Poliwag. Energetic. Eager.

Koda and Meadow exchanged a glance.

"Arcanine and Bulbasaur," Koda said.

Meadow nodded. "Keep it grounded."

Psyduck opened with Confusion, targeting Arcanine. Poliwag sent a rolling Water Gun sweeping wide, trying to split them apart.

Arcanine took the Confusion hit and steadied. Bulbasaur's vines shot low and wrapped Poliwag before it could redirect the water stream.

"Arcanine — Ember, gentle. Don't overwhelm."

A controlled burst pushed Psyduck back without hurting it badly. Koda kept his wind entirely still — this was Arcanine's moment to read the field.

Bulbasaur used Vine Whip twice in quick succession, patient and precise, until Poliwag sat down exhausted.

Psyduck lasted a little longer — headache working against it as much as anything — before Arcanine's final Flamethrower ended it cleanly.

The siblings cheered anyway. Good sports.

"You two move like you've been training together for years," the older one said.

Meadow glanced at Koda. "Something like that."
