# Bolt's Brave Button

The last piece of the map was inside a ring of floating space rocks.

The *Pebble* flew slowly. Left. Right. Up. Down.

*Bonk!*

A rock bumped the ship. The *Pebble* stopped.

"We're stuck," said {{hero}}. "A rock is caught on the outside."

"*Someone has to go outside and push it off,*" said {{mom}} on the radio.

The boys tried the little side door. They were too big to fit!

Slowly, everybody looked at Bolt.

"Oh no," said Bolt. His eyes blinked very fast. "I'm the scared one. Remember?"

"You don't have to," said {{hero}} kindly.

Bolt was quiet. Then he opened a little door on his tummy. Inside was a red button. It said: *BRAVE*.

"If I press this," Bolt whispered, "maybe I won't be scared. But what if I press it and I'm *still* scared?"

{{brother}} thought about the dark. And thunder. And bees.

"Being brave doesn't mean not being scared," said {{brother}}. "It means being scared, and doing it anyway."

Bolt looked at the button.

Then he closed the little door. He didn't press it.

"I'll do it the *real* way," he said.

He clipped on his safety rope. He took a big wobbly breath. And out he went.

The boys pressed their noses to the window. Bolt was shaking like jelly. But he kept going. Step. Step. Step.

He reached the rock. He gave it a big push.

*Wheee!* The *Pebble* floated free!

When Bolt came back inside, he was holding a piece of glowing blue paper.

The fourth piece! Now the map was whole. It showed the way to the Hidden Star!

"I was scared the whole time," said Bolt.

"We know," said {{hero}}, giving him a big hug. "That's what made it brave."
