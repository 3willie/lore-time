# Skye's Sore Wing

*Crunch!*

Skye had landed on a sharp rock. Now she was holding up one wing. It hurt.

"Her wing is bruised," said Master Oona. "She mustn't fly for a while. Maybe two weeks."

"Two weeks!" said {{hero}}. "But I want to fly!"

Then he looked at Skye. She looked sad and sore.

{{hero}} felt bad. Skye always carried *him*. Now it was his turn to carry her. Well, not *carry*. But help.

"What does she need?" he asked.

"Rest," said Master Oona. "Cool cloths on the bruise. And company. Dragons get lonely when they can't fly."

So every day, {{hero}} looked after Skye.

In the morning, he brought her a big bucket of fish.

At lunchtime, he put cool, wet cloths on her wing. *Ahh.* Skye's eyes closed.

In the afternoon, he brushed her scales until they shone.

Sometimes {{hero}} wanted to go play with the others. They were flying, and laughing, and racing. But he stayed.

"You're missing the fun," said {{brother}}.

"Skye would stay with me," said {{hero}}.

{{brother}} thought about that. Then he and Cinder stayed too.

Every evening, they sat with Skye and watched the sunset. Cinder hiccupped little smoke rings to make her laugh.

After two weeks, Master Oona checked the wing.

"All better," she said.

Skye stretched her wing. Wide, wide, wide. She flapped once. Twice.

Then she lowered her shoulder for {{hero}} to climb on.

*WHOOSH!*

Up, up into the clouds.

Skye did a loop. A happy one. Just for him.
