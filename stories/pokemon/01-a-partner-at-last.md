# A Partner at Last

Sam had waited all year for this day. Today, he would choose his very first Pokémon.

The lab smelled of cleaning spray and something sweet, like berries. Three Poké Balls sat on the table.

"Take your time," said the Professor. "A partner is for a long journey."

Sam's hand hovered over the first ball. Then the second. Then — *pop!* — before he could touch anything, the third ball wobbled, burst open, and a small yellow Pokémon tumbled out onto the floor.

"Pika?"

It was a Pikachu, and it looked very surprised to be there.

The Professor laughed. "Well! That one doesn't usually come out on its own."

Sam knelt down slowly and held out his hand. Pikachu sniffed it. Then its cheeks crackled, and — *zzzt!* — a tiny shock made Sam's hair stand straight up.

"Hey!" Sam said. But then he started to giggle, and Pikachu's ears twitched, and soon they were both laughing.

"It's a bit wild," said the Professor. "It might not listen at first."

"That's okay," said Sam. "I'm not great at listening either."

Sam didn't put Pikachu back in its ball. He let it ride on his shoulder all the way home. It sniffed every flower and growled at a pigeon and stole half of his sandwich.

At the end of the day, as the sun went down, Pikachu curled up on Sam's pillow.

"We're going to be a good team," Sam whispered. "We'll learn together."

"Pika," said Pikachu sleepily, which Sam decided meant *yes*.
