# The Big Huddle

That night, it got cold. Very, very cold.

Inside the *Pebble*, something went *clunk*.

"Uh-oh," said Bolt. "The heater has stopped."

"I can fix it," said {{hero}}. He was the captain, after all.

He got the tools. He turned a bolt. He wiggled a wire. He pressed all the buttons.

The heater stayed cold. {{brother}}'s teeth went *chatter, chatter*.

"Maybe we should ask for help," said Bolt.

"Captains don't need help," said {{hero}}. He tried again. His fingers were getting stiff.

There was a tap at the door. *Tap, tap.*

It was Pip. "Why is your ship shivering?"

"Our heater broke," said {{brother}}.

"Why didn't you say so?" said Pip. "Come with us!"

{{hero}} looked at his cold hands. He looked at his shivering brother.

"Okay," he said. "We need help. Please."

Outside, all the penguins were standing close together in a great big circle.

"This is the Huddle," said Mrs. Flap. "Nobody stays warm alone on Snow Planet. Squeeze in!"

So they did. The penguins were soft and fluffy. It was as cozy as a blanket.

"When you're warm, you move to the outside," said Pip. "Then someone cold gets a turn in the middle."

In the morning, Mrs. Flap looked at the heater. She had fixed lots of heaters.

"Just a bit of ice in the pipe," she said. *Tap!* It hummed again.

"I should have asked sooner," said {{hero}}.

"Now you know," said Mrs. Flap. "Even captains need a huddle."
