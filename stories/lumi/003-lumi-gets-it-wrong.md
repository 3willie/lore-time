# Lumi Gets It Wrong

{{brother}} was drawing a penguin.

"Lumi," he asked, "can penguins fly?"

*Blink, blink.* "Yes," said Lumi. "Penguins are birds, and birds fly."

So {{brother}} drew his penguin high in the sky, next to the sun.

{{hero}} looked at the picture. He frowned.

"I don't think that's right," he said. "I think penguins swim."

"Lumi said they fly," said {{brother}}. "And Lumi knows everything."

"Let's check," said {{hero}}.

They found {{dad}}, and {{dad}} found the big animal book. They turned the pages. *Flip, flip, flip.* Lions. Monkeys. Owls. And then…

Penguins!

"*Penguins cannot fly,*" read {{dad}}. "*Their wings are flippers. They swim fast, like fish.*"

{{brother}}'s mouth fell open.

They went back to the kitchen. "Lumi," said {{hero}}, "penguins don't fly. They swim. We looked in a book."

*Blink, blink, blink.*

"You're right," said Lumi. "I made a mistake. I'm sorry. Thank you for checking."

"But you're so smart," said {{brother}}. "How can you be wrong?"

"I know a lot," said Lumi. "But I don't know everything. Sometimes I mix things up. That's why checking is a very good idea."

"Who should we check with?" asked {{brother}}.

"A grown-up. A book. Or your own two eyes," said Lumi.

{{brother}} looked at his drawing. Then he got a blue crayon and drew water all around his penguin.

"Now he's not flying," he said. "He's doing a really big jump."

"Splash!" said Lumi.
