# The Messy Planet

"*Rangers,*" said {{mom}}, "*a little planet called Bim-Bam needs help. I'm not sure why. Go and see.*"

The *Pebble* landed. *Crunch. Crinkle. Crash.*

They had landed on a pile of junk!

Bim-Bam was covered in it. Old boxes. Broken toys. Wrappers. Bottles. Socks with no friends.

A small green creature peeked out of a cereal box.

"Welcome to Bim-Bam," he said sadly. "I'm Mo. We used to have flowers. And a pond. Now we just have… stuff."

"Where did it all come from?" asked {{hero}}.

"Everybody just drops things," said Mo. "And nobody picks them up. It's somebody else's job."

"Whose job is it?" asked {{brother}}.

Mo shrugged. "Nobody's."

{{hero}} thought. "Maybe it's *everybody's* job."

So they started. Just the three of them, and Mo.

Bolt sorted things. "Bottles here. Paper there. Toys in this box." He was *very* good at sorting.

{{brother}} found the lonely socks and matched them up. "Now they have friends!"

{{hero}} and Mo carried the big boxes.

Other green creatures peeked out to watch. One came over to help. Then three. Then twenty!

Under the junk, they found something wonderful. Green grass. Little yellow flowers. And a pond, shining blue.

"I forgot it was there," whispered Mo.

They made a special corner for old things. Some things could be used again. Some could be made into new things. The broken toys got fixed.

"What if it gets messy again?" asked {{brother}}.

Mo grinned. "Now it's everybody's job," he said.

And he picked up a wrapper, right then and there.
