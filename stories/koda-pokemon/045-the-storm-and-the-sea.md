# The Storm and the Sea

The afternoon sun hung low over the coastline when Koda spotted the wild Lapras drifting near the rocky shore. Its shell gleamed like polished ice, and its long neck swayed gently above the waves.

"Arcanine, stay back," Koda murmured, his fingers already moving to his belt. He'd been feeling something unusual in the air since morning — a deep, cold pressure, like a storm held underwater. *Lapras,* he thought. *And it's unsettled.*

He reached for Pikachu's ball, then paused. Pikachu was already sitting on his shoulder, ears pricked forward, golden eyes locked on the sea creature.

"You feel it too?" Koda whispered.

Pikachu dropped to the ground and stepped forward without a word. Some battles didn't need to be called.

Lapras opened its mouth and released a spinning **Ice Beam** — a bright, crackling ribbon of cold that raked across the sand. Pikachu leapt sideways, skidded low, and fired back with **Thunderbolt**. The bolt cracked against Lapras's shell and the great Pokémon bellowed, stepping backward into deeper water.

Then the sea began to glow.

A pale, roaring light erupted from beneath the surface. The water surged upward in curtains, and Lapras *grew* — its shell expanding like a sail catching hurricane wind, fins spreading wide as boat hulls, neck rising until its head blocked the sun. The **Gigantamax energy** rolled off it in waves of freezing mist, coating the sand in frost.

Koda's breath fogged. He felt the cold in his chest but kept his feet planted.

"Pikachu." His voice was steady. "You've got this."

Pikachu stood small against that enormous silhouette — ears flat, cheeks sparking white. Then it inhaled, planted all four paws, and unleashed a **Max Lightning** so bright it turned the whole coastline silver. The bolt spiraled up, split, and crashed into Lapras like a breaking wave in reverse.

The glow faded slowly.

Lapras shrank back to its true size, swaying in the shallows, breathing hard. But it wasn't afraid anymore. The tension that had been coiled in its eyes — that cold, restless anger — had gone quiet.

Pikachu walked to the waterline and sat down.

Lapras lowered its head until its chin nearly touched the waves, studying the little yellow Pokémon for a long moment. Then, gently, it nuzzled forward.

Koda let out a breath he didn't know he'd been holding.

*Sometimes,* he thought, *the battle isn't really a battle at all.*
