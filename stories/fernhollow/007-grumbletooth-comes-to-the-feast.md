# Grumbletooth Comes to the Feast

The feast was just beginning when the ground began to shake.

*STOMP. STOMP. STOMP.*

Every dinosaur froze.

Out of the trees came Old Grumbletooth.

The Zippers zipped under a bush. The triceratops backed away. Spike tucked in his tail. Even Tallulah lifted her head up high, out of reach.

Old Grumbletooth stopped at the edge of the clearing. He looked at all the dinosaurs moving away from him.

He was carrying something in his mouth. He set it down on the grass.

It was a watermelon. A big, round, stripy one.

Nobody moved.

Old Grumbletooth looked at his feet. He began to turn around.

"Wait!" said {{hero}}.

His tummy felt fluttery. But he walked out, right across the clearing. {{brother}} ran to catch up and held his hand.

"Hello, Grumbletooth," said {{hero}}. "How's your foot?"

The big T. rex lifted his foot and wiggled it. All better.

"Is that for the feast?" asked {{brother}}, pointing at the watermelon.

Old Grumbletooth nudged it toward them with his nose.

"He brought something to share," said {{hero}} loudly, so everyone could hear. "He was only grumpy because of the thorn. He's nice now. Come and see!"

Toot was the first. He trotted over and went *toot!*

Old Grumbletooth went *hrrrmmm*. That was a hello.

One by one, the others crept back.

Then Old Grumbletooth put one big foot on the watermelon.

*SPLAT!*

It cracked into lots of juicy pieces. Enough for everybody!

"Best thing at the feast," said Ranger Juno.

Grumbletooth looked very pleased.
