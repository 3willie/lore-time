# The Long Flight

"Riders, up!" called Master Oona.

Today they would fly to the Ember Isles. It was a long, long way. All across the sea.

{{hero}} climbed onto Skye. {{brother}} climbed onto Cinder.

*Flap, flap, WHOOSH!*

Up they went. Past the castle. Past the clouds. Out over the sparkling blue water.

Skye flew smooth and strong. Cinder flapped very hard to keep up. *Flap-flap-flap-flap!*

After a while, Cinder began to puff. *Huff. Puff.*

"Are you okay?" asked {{brother}}.

Cinder nodded. He didn't want to be the slow one.

*Huff. Puff. Hic.*

He dipped lower. And lower. His tail almost touched the waves!

"Cinder's tired," called {{brother}}. "But he won't stop!"

{{hero}} leaned forward. "Skye," he said, "can we help?"

Skye slowed down. She flew right in front of Cinder, with her big wings spread wide.

"Tuck in behind her!" called Master Oona. "Her wings push the wind away. It's easier there."

Cinder tucked in. Oh! It *was* easier. Like being pulled along.

"And there's a rock ahead," said Master Oona. "We'll all stop for a snack."

They landed on a little island. Cinder flopped down. He ate six fish. Then he had a nap, with his head on Skye's tail.

"Even the biggest dragons rest," Master Oona told {{brother}}. "Resting isn't losing. It's how you get there."

When Cinder woke up, he stretched his wings.

*Hic!* A happy little flame.

"Ready?" asked {{brother}}.

Cinder was ready.

And far ahead, the Ember Isles glowed orange in the sun.
