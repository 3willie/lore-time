# Five Roads With Athena 4: Rival Trainers

The north road narrowed through a stretch of dense woodland, and somewhere inside it they found two trainers mid-argument about whose Pokémon was stronger. The argument had apparently been ongoing for some time and had reached the stage where only an actual battle would settle it.

They both turned to Koda and Athena at the same time.

"Judge our battle," the first one said.

"Absolutely not," the second said. "Battle us instead. Prove which type is better — Grass or Normal."

Koda looked at the Pokémon in question — a Bellsprout and a Fearow respectively. Both well-trained. Both genuinely good.

"One each," he said.

---

Athena took Bellsprout. Koda took Fearow.

Athena's Eevee against Bellsprout was a study in patience — Bellsprout was technically faster and had type reach, but Eevee moved with an instinctive quality that kept it just outside every committed vine strike. Athena barely spoke. Eevee navigated the battle almost entirely on its own read of the moment, Swift wearing Bellsprout down in clean measured bursts.

Koda watched it while managing Fearow with Dragonair.

Fearow came in hard and aerial — good instincts, using height and speed. Dragonair met it without escalating, Thunder Wave clipping it on the third pass and grounding its speed advantage. One Dragon Rage ended it with more power than Fearow had been expecting from a Dragon type at this stage.

Fearow sat down looking genuinely surprised.

Both trainers were quiet for a moment.

"So who won the argument?" the first one asked.

"Neither of you," Koda said. "Your Pokémon are both good. The argument was the wrong question."

The second trainer frowned. Then slowly — grudgingly — nodded.

Athena was already walking.

Koda caught up.

"You're getting philosophical," she said.

"It seemed like the right moment."

"It was," she admitted.
