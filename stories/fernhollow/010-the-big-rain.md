# The Big Rain

It rained and rained and rained in Fernhollow.

*Drip, drip. Splash, splash. WHOOSH.*

The river grew bigger. And bigger. Soon it was so wide and so fast that nobody could cross it.

"Oh no," said Ranger Juno. "The baby triceratops are on the other side. And their mother is over here!"

Across the river, three little triceratops were huddled under a tree. They looked wet and scared. Their mother paced up and down the bank, calling. *Mmmmm! Mmmmm!*

"How can we get them across?" asked {{brother}}.

"The water's too fast to swim," said Juno.

{{hero}} looked up. Way up. At Tallulah's long, long neck.

"Tallulah!" he called. "Can you help?"

Tallulah walked to the edge of the river. She stretched her long neck all the way across, and rested her chin on the other side.

It was a bridge! A dinosaur bridge!

"But the babies are too scared to walk on it," said {{brother}}.

"Toot," said {{hero}}. "Can you show them?"

Toot was scared too. But he took a breath and trotted across Tallulah's neck. Carefully. One foot, then the other.

*Toot!* he called from the other side. *Come on!*

The first baby followed him. Then the second. Then the third.

Old Grumbletooth stood in the river below, like a big wall, in case anyone slipped.

Spike swept his tail to push the floating branches away.

Even the little Zippers helped. They ran back and forth, squeaking, cheering everyone on.

One by one, all three babies made it across.

Their mother licked their faces. *Mmmmm.*

"Everybody helped," said {{brother}}.

"Everyone in their own way," said Juno.
