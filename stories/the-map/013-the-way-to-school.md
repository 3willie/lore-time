# The Way to School

The back of the map had a new path. It went out of the gate, down the street, around two corners… and stopped at school.

*The Way to School*, said the tiny letters.

"But we walk to school every day," said {{brother}}. "That's not special."

"Maybe the map wants us to look closer," said {{hero}}.

So the next morning, they walked to school with {{dad}}. And they looked. Really looked.

At the corner stood Mr. Pete, the crossing guard. He wore a bright yellow vest. He held up his big STOP sign so cars would wait.

"He's here every day," said {{hero}}. "Even when it rains."

"Even when it snows," said {{brother}}.

They passed the bakery. Mrs. Lin was putting bread in the window. She waved.

They passed a man picking up trash in the park, so it stayed nice for everyone.

At the school gate, Mrs. Garcia unlocked the door and said "Good morning!" to every single kid.

"There are so many helpers," said {{brother}}. "I never noticed."

"Let's say thank you," said {{hero}}.

The next day, they brought drawings.

For Mr. Pete, a picture of him in his yellow vest, holding his sign.

For Mrs. Lin, a picture of a giant loaf of bread.

For Mrs. Garcia, a picture of the school with a big sun on top.

Mr. Pete held his drawing for a long time.

"In twenty years," he said, "nobody ever drew me before." He put it in his vest pocket, right over his heart.

That night, the map had drawn a tiny yellow vest on the corner.

And a heart.
