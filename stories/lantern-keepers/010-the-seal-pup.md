# The Seal Pup

*Aroo! Aroo!*

Something was crying down on the rocks.

The boys ran to look. It was a baby seal! It was gray and round, with big dark eyes. It was all alone.

"Where's your mommy?" asked {{brother}}.

*Aroo,* said the seal pup sadly.

"She's lost her mother in the fog," said Old Wick. "And the mother has lost her."

"Turn on a lamp!" said {{hero}}.

They lit the green lamp. But that one is for boats.

They lit the blue lamp. But that one is for birds.

No mother seal came.

The pup shivered. So {{brother}} took off his sweater and tucked it around her. {{hero}} sat close beside her, so she wouldn't be lonely. He hummed a little song.

"We'll stay with you," he said.

Up in the kitchen window, something went *flicker*.

"Look!" said {{brother}}.

It was the little red lamp! It was glowing, warm and rosy, like a sunset.

"Well, I never," whispered Nell. "It lights for *kindness*."

The red light spread out over the water. It wasn't bright. But it was warm. Anyone who saw it knew: *somebody kind is here.*

Out in the fog, a shiny gray head popped up. Then it swam closer. And closer.

*AROO!*

The mother seal!

The pup wiggled out of the sweater and flopped into the sea. *Splash!* The two seals bumped noses. Then they swam away together.

"Bye!" called {{brother}}. His sweater was wet and smelled like fish.

He didn't mind one bit.
