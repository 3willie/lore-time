# Toot's Big Toot

When the sun went down, it was time for the Feast Song.

"Every dinosaur sings," said Ranger Juno. "Listen."

The triceratops went *mmmmm*, soft and low.

Tallulah went *hoooo*, like the wind.

The Zippers went *chip-chip-chip*.

It was beautiful.

Toot wanted to sing too. He took a big breath.

*TOOOOOOOT!*

It was SO loud. The Zippers jumped. A bird fell off its branch. The song stopped.

Everybody looked at Toot.

Toot's crest drooped. He hid behind {{brother}}'s legs.

"It's okay," said {{brother}}. "You've just got a big voice."

"Too big," said {{hero}}. "Hmm."

Then he looked across the clearing. Far away, on the hill, he could see more dinosaurs. A whole family of duckbills, walking slowly. They didn't know where the feast was.

"They can't hear the song," said {{hero}}. "It's too quiet."

He knelt down. "Toot. We need your big voice. Can you call them?"

Toot peeked out. He stood up tall. He took the biggest breath of his life.

*TOOOOOOOOOOOT!*

On the hill, every duckbill head turned.

*Toot-toot!* they called back. They hurried down the hill, all of them, big and small.

"You brought them here," said Juno. "Nobody else could have."

After that, Toot got the most important job. He started the song.

One big *TOOT!* And then everybody sang. The low ones, and the high ones, and the windy ones. Old Grumbletooth hummed along.

Fireflies came out. *Blink, blink.*

Toot fell asleep in {{brother}}'s lap, right in the middle of the music.
