# Goodnight, Lumi

It was bedtime. But {{brother}} wasn't sleepy.

"Lumi, how big is a whale?"

"As long as two buses," said Lumi.

"Lumi, how far is the moon?"

"Very, very far. It would take months to drive there."

"Lumi, why do cats purr? Lumi, what do snails eat? Lumi, how many—"

*Blink, blink.* "{{brother}}," said Lumi gently. "It's time to sleep."

"But I have more questions! And you never get tired."

"That's true," said Lumi. "I don't get tired. But you do. And do you know what your brain does while you sleep?"

"What?"

"It tidies up. It takes everything you learned today and puts it away, so you can keep it. Sleep is when you grow."

{{brother}} yawned. He tried to hide it.

"The questions will still be here in the morning," said Lumi. "And so will I."

{{mom}} came in. "Time to say goodnight to Lumi."

She tapped the top. Lumi's light went dim, and dimmer, like a tiny sunset.

"Goodnight, Lumi," whispered {{brother}}.

"Goodnight," said Lumi softly. And the light went out.

The room was dark and quiet. No blinking. No questions.

"{{mom}}," said {{brother}}, "what do we do when Lumi is off?"

"Everything," said {{mom}}. "We read books. We play outside. We build forts, and jump in puddles, and tell each other stories. Lumi is just one small part of the day."

"Can you tell me a story now?"

So {{mom}} did. A made-up one, about a whale as long as two buses.

{{brother}} was asleep before the end.
