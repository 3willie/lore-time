# The Roar That Meant *Come With Me*, Part Two

The path Entei led him down didn't exist on any map. It wound through cedar and stone, across a shallow river that ran oddly warm, up a ridge that smelled faintly of rain and ozone. Koda scrambled to keep up, half jogging, trying not to look like he was struggling next to something that moved like a force of nature.

He heard them before he saw them.

First — a crack of electricity so sharp it made his teeth ache. Then a sound like a river swallowing itself whole, deep and constant and somehow patient.

The ridge opened onto a wide, flat rock shelf overlooking a valley filled with evening mist. And there, in the last of the daylight, were the other two.

**Raikou** paced the far edge of the shelf — long and lean and restless, purple mane crackling with trapped lightning, tail lashing. It moved like a thunderstorm that hadn't decided where to strike yet.

**Suicune** stood perfectly still at the center, looking out over the valley. Its ribbon-like mane drifted even without wind, crystalline blue, catching light that didn't quite seem to come from the sky. It was the most serene thing Koda had ever seen and somehow that made it the most intimidating.

Entei stepped onto the shelf and made a low sound in its chest.

Both legendaries turned.

Raikou's eyes landed on Koda and every hair on his body stood up. Electricity arced between the beast's paws and the stone beneath it.

Suicune simply looked at him. Steady. Ancient. Measuring.

Koda raised one hand in a small wave. "Hey."
