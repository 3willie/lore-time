# Think First

{{brother}} liked asking Lumi things.

"Lumi, which shoe goes on which foot?"

"Lumi, what comes after six?"

"Lumi, where's my blue truck?"

*Blink, blink.* "I don't know where your truck is," said Lumi. "I can't see under your bed."

"It's under my bed?" {{brother}} ran to look. It was!

"Lumi, what's two apples and one more apple?"

*Blink, blink.*

"What do *you* think?" asked Lumi.

"I don't know. You tell me."

"I could," said Lumi. "But your brain is like a muscle. It gets stronger when you use it. Do you want a strong brain?"

{{brother}} did want a strong brain.

"Then here's a game," said Lumi. "It's called Think First. You try. If you get stuck, I'll give you a clue."

{{brother}} got two apples from the bowl. He got one more. He touched each one.

"One. Two. THREE!"

"You did it," said Lumi. "I didn't help at all."

{{brother}} stood up very tall.

He looked at his shoes. He thought first. The curvy sides go together in the middle… He put them on.

"Right feet!" said {{hero}}.

"What comes after six?" said {{brother}} to himself. "One, two, three, four, five, six… SEVEN!"

All day, he played Think First. Sometimes he got it. Sometimes he needed a clue. Lumi always had one ready.

At bedtime, {{mom}} asked, "What did Lumi teach you today?"

"Nothing!" said {{brother}} proudly. "I did it with my own brain."

Lumi's light glowed extra bright.
