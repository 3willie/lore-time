# The Marsh Badge and What Comes After, Part One

Athena was sitting on the gym steps when Koda pushed through the doors, Eevee in her lap, journal open but not being written in. She looked up at him and read the badge case before he said a word.

"Five," she said.

"Five." He sat beside her on the steps. "Sabrina wants to battle you."

Athena looked at the gym doors.

"She said that?"

"She sensed you when we arrived. Said your ability is genuine." He paused. "Her word."

Athena was quiet for a moment — not nervous, processing. Eevee looked up at her and she scratched its ears absently.

"She's psychic," Athena said. "She already knows what I can do."

"Probably."

"That's either an advantage or a significant problem."

"Go find out."

She closed her journal, set Eevee gently beside Koda, and stood.

She looked at the doors for one more second.

Then went in.

---

Koda sat on the steps with Eevee and waited.

He let Arcanine out to rest in the afternoon sun, still tired from Alakazam. Dragonair coiled loosely on the warm stone. Kadabra sat beside him in the particular way it had developed of being present without demanding anything.

Eevee watched the gym doors with focused attention the entire time.

*It knows,* Koda thought. *It's tracking her through the walls somehow.*

---

Inside — he couldn't see it, but he pieced it together later from what Athena told him that evening.

Sabrina had met her at the chamber entrance and done something she apparently didn't do for other challengers.

She'd spoken first.

*"You don't command them. You listen to them. That's rarer than any technique."*

Athena had said — *"Is that going to make this easier or harder?"*

*"Harder,"* Sabrina had said. *"My Pokémon will trust you. They'll fight harder because of it."*
