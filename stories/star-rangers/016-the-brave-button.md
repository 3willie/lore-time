# The Brave Button

Bolt was polishing his badge. The one that said *bravest robot in the fleet*.

He opened the little door on his tummy. There was the red button. *BRAVE*.

He had never pressed it. Not once.

"Bolt," said {{brother}}. "What does it *do*?"

"I don't know," said Bolt. "My builder gave it to me. I always thought, one day, I'll be scared enough to need it."

"You've been scared lots of times," said {{hero}}. "In the space rocks. In the Nebula. With the baby penguin."

"And I never pressed it," said Bolt. He looked at the button. "Maybe I should find out what it does."

"Are you scared to press it?" asked {{brother}}.

Bolt's eyes blinked. "A little."

"We'll be right here," said {{hero}}.

Bolt took a deep, wobbly breath. He reached for the button.

*Click.*

There was a soft whirr. Then a voice came out of Bolt's chest. A warm, kind, old voice.

"*Hello, Bolt. It's me. The one who built you.*"

Bolt's eyes went very wide.

"*If you're hearing this, you were scared and you wanted help. But I want to tell you a secret. This button doesn't do anything at all. It never did. The brave part was never in the button.*"

The voice chuckled.

"*It was always in you. I knew it the day I built you.*"

The voice stopped.

Nobody said anything for a long time.

"It was in me," Bolt whispered. "All along."

"We knew that," said {{brother}}.

"You did?"

"Yep," said {{hero}}. "You were brave every time. You just didn't know it yet."

Bolt closed the little door. And this time, he smiled.
