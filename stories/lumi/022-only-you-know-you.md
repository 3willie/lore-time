# Only You Know You

"Lumi," said {{brother}}, "guess my favorite color."

*Blink, blink.* "Lots of children like blue," said Lumi. "So I'll guess blue."

"WRONG!" laughed {{brother}}. "It's green!"

"Ah," said Lumi. "I made a guess. But only you know your favorite color."

"Guess what I'm thinking right now," said {{brother}}.

*Blink, blink.* "I can't," said Lumi. "I can't see inside your head. Nobody can."

{{brother}} thought about that. It was a strange idea.

"Not even {{mom}}?"

"Not even {{mom}}," said Lumi. "She knows you very well. But your thoughts and feelings belong to you."

Later, {{brother}} felt sad. He didn't know why. He just did.

"Lumi," he said, "why am I sad?"

*Blink, blink.* "I don't know," said Lumi gently. "You're the only one who can feel it. Can you tell me what it feels like?"

{{brother}} thought. "Like a gray cloud. In my tummy."

"That's a good way to say it," said Lumi. "Who could you tell about the gray cloud?"

"{{hero}}," said {{brother}}.

So he found his brother. "I've got a gray cloud in my tummy."

{{hero}} sat down next to him. "Do you want to tell me about it?"

{{brother}} told him. He missed Gull Rock. He missed Ember. He missed the sea.

"Me too," said {{hero}}. And he got out his pocket lantern.

They thought about Gull Rock together.

The lanterns went *twinkle*.

The gray cloud got smaller.

"Lumi," said {{brother}} later. "Only *I* know what I feel. But I can tell people."

"Yes," said Lumi. "That's the most important part."
