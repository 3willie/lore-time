# Brenna's Bad Day

Brenna was the best rider at Highcrag. She had won the golden ribbon. Her dragon, Flash, was the fastest of all.

But today, Brenna was sitting in the stables, crying.

{{brother}} saw her first. "What's wrong?"

"Go away," said Brenna.

{{brother}} wanted to go away. Brenna was sometimes a bit bossy.

But {{hero}} sat down on a hay bale nearby. Not too close. Just there.

After a while, Brenna said, "Flash is sick. He won't eat. He won't fly. He just lies there."

They looked. Flash was curled up, with his head down. His bright green scales looked dull.

"Master Oona says he's got a dragon cold," said Brenna. "He'll get better. But he has to rest for a whole week." Her voice cracked. "And it's the Sky Race on Saturday. I can't fly."

"That's really sad," said {{hero}}.

"It's not fair," said Brenna.

"It's not," agreed {{brother}}.

They didn't try to fix it. They just stayed.

Then {{brother}} had an idea. "When I'm sick, {{mom}} reads me stories. Maybe Flash would like that?"

Brenna sniffed. "Dragons don't like stories."

"Cinder does," said {{brother}}.

So {{brother}} told a story. It was about a tiny elephant who lived in a hat. Cinder curled up to listen. Then — slowly — Flash lifted his head.

By the end, Flash's tail was wagging, just a little.

Brenna laughed. Her first laugh all day.

"Will you come back tomorrow?" she asked. "And tell another one?"

"Every day," said {{brother}}. "Until he's better."

And they did.
