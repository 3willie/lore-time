# The Last Mile

Morning came in gray and soft, the kind that made the forest feel close and quiet. Koda packed camp without rushing, watching Dratini glide through the low mist that had settled overnight along the trail.

It was moving differently again.

Looser. More deliberate. Like yesterday's battle had settled something inside it that had been waiting to settle.

*Good,* Koda thought. *Let it sit with that.*

---

They were two hours into the day's walk when Arcanine stopped.

Ears forward. Nose low.

Koda read the air immediately — something large, moving fast, coming through the canopy from the northeast. Not threatening exactly. Panicked.

A Fearow burst through the upper branches, spiraling badly, one wing held at a wrong angle. It crashed into a mossy slope twenty feet ahead and skidded to a stop against a fallen log.

It looked up at Koda's team with wild frightened eyes and shrieked — warning them off even now, hurt and cornered.

Koda stopped his team with one hand.

*Don't crowd it.*

He moved forward alone, slowly, and crouched at a distance that wasn't threatening.

The wing was bent — not broken, he thought. Strained badly. Something had hit it hard up in the canopy.

"Nobody's going to bother you," he said quietly.

Fearow snapped its beak at the air between them.

Koda didn't move back.

He let the air around Fearow settle — gently, the way he'd done at the pool with Dratini. A slow calm pressure that said *this space is safe.*

Fearow's beak closed. It watched him.

He reached into his bag for the small medical kit he carried — had used it twice already on his own team — and set the kit on the ground between them. Opened it. Didn't reach forward.

*Your choice.*

Fearow looked at the kit. Looked at him.

Then it shuffled forward half a foot, wing dragging.

Koda worked carefully and without rushing — a stabilizing wrap, a small application of potion along the joint. Fearow flinched twice but held still, watching his hands the whole time.

When he finished he sat back.

Fearow tested the wing slowly. Not fixed — but better. Manageable.

It looked at him for a long moment with sharp intelligent eyes.

Then it pushed off the log and climbed back into the canopy, labored but steady, disappearing through the branches.

Koda sat still a moment longer.

Pidgeot landed beside him and looked up through the gap in the canopy where the Fearow had gone.

"Yeah," Koda said. "I know."

---

They reached the edge of the forest by early afternoon. The trees thinned and the path widened and through the last gap in the branches Koda saw rooftops and the distant shape of a city spreading out below a gray-blue sky.

He stopped at the tree line and looked back into the forest once.

Somewhere in there wild Pokémon were moving through their day — cautious and free, unbothered.

The way it should be.

He turned forward.

"Next badge," he said to no one in particular.

Arcanine fell into step. Dratini glided alongside. Kadabra drifted. Pidgeot lifted off the branch above and circled once before climbing toward the open sky.

Koda walked out of the trees and into the light.
