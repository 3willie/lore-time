# Brushing Grumbletooth's Teeth

Old Grumbletooth came to the cabin. He opened his giant mouth.

*Hrrrmmm,* he groaned.

"Is it another thorn?" asked {{brother}}.

Ranger Juno climbed up a ladder and looked inside. She held her nose.

"Phew! No thorn," she said. "It's his teeth. They're covered in yucky stuff. He's never brushed them. Not once!"

"NEVER?" said {{hero}}. "Not even once?"

"Dinosaurs don't usually brush," said Juno. "But Grumbletooth eats a lot of sticky fruit now. And sticky fruit makes sore teeth."

"We brush our teeth every day," said {{brother}} proudly. "Two whole minutes."

Juno smiled. "Then you're the experts. Can you help?"

So they made a giant toothbrush. It was a broom, with the bristles trimmed short.

"Open wide, Grumbletooth!" said {{hero}}.

Grumbletooth opened wide. His teeth were as big as bananas!

"Start at the back," said {{brother}}. "That's where the yucky stuff hides."

{{hero}} brushed the top teeth. *Scrub, scrub, scrub.*

{{brother}} brushed the bottom teeth. *Scrub, scrub, scrub.*

"Little circles!" said {{brother}}. "That's how you do it."

"Don't forget the front!"

"Don't forget the sides!"

Toot ran back and forth with buckets of water. *Toot! Toot!*

"Now rinse!" said Juno.

Grumbletooth took a big gulp of water and swished it around. *Gargle, gargle.* Then — *SPLOOSH!* — he spat it into the river.

He grinned. His teeth were shiny white.

*Hrrrmmm!* That was a happy hrrrmmm.

"Same time tomorrow?" said {{hero}}.

Grumbletooth nodded.

That night, the boys brushed their own teeth extra well. Little circles. Back, front and sides.

Just like experts.
