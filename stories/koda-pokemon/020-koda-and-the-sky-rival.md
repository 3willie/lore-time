# Koda and the Sky Rival

The road out of Cerulean City wound through open fields and rolling hills, the kind of place where the wind moved freely and Koda could breathe easy. He walked with Arcanine at his side, Kadabra floating quietly a few steps behind.

He was heading for Vermilion City. Third badge on his mind.

Then the air shifted.

Koda felt it before he saw it — a sharp, curving pressure high above. He looked up just as a Pidgeot burst through the clouds, banking hard, sending a gust so powerful it flattened the grass in a wide ring around them.

Arcanine planted his feet. Kadabra's eyes sharpened.

"That's no wild gust," Koda said quietly. "That's a challenge."

Pidgeot circled back, locked eyes with him, and dove.

"Arcanine — Flamethrower, cut across its path!"

Arcanine roared and sent a sweeping arc of fire across the sky. Pidgeot twisted, barely dodging, and answered with a Hurricane that sent Koda sliding back two steps.

*Fast. Really fast.*

"Kadabra — Psybeam, lead it left!"

Kadabra fired a precise beam, not at Pidgeot — *ahead* of it, forcing a direction change. Pidgeot veered right, directly into Arcanine's follow-up Ember. The hit connected. Pidgeot faltered — then steadied, climbing again, proud and fierce.

Koda smiled. *It's strong. That's exactly why.*

"Arcanine, one more — Extreme Speed, close the distance. Then stop."

Arcanine launched forward in a blur, stopping just beneath Pidgeot's flight path — not attacking, just *present.* A show of respect.

Pidgeot hovered. Watching.

Koda reached into his bag and threw the Poké Ball cleanly.

It clicked once. Twice. Three times.

Still.

He walked over and picked it up slowly, feeling the calm inside.

"You fought well," he said. "We all did."

Kadabra gave a quiet nod. Arcanine huffed warmly.

Koda clipped the Poké Ball to his belt and looked toward the horizon. Vermilion City was still a long walk away.

But now they had the sky on their side.
