# The Vermilion Challenge, Part Two

## Raichu and the Thunder Badge

Raichu didn't move immediately.

It stood in the center of the gym floor and *waited* — confident, coiled, watching Pidgeot with dark calm eyes. Like it had done this a hundred times and already knew how it ended.

Surge said nothing. He didn't need to.

*It's reading Pidgeot,* Koda realized. *Waiting for a committed move.*

"Pidgeot — Gust, wide. Don't commit to a direction."

Pidgeot beat its wings and sent a broad unfocused gust across the floor. Not an attack — a disruption. Papers scattered. Raichu's footing shifted slightly.

Raichu launched forward in a blur — Quick Attack, closing the distance before Pidgeot could redirect. The hit landed hard. Pidgeot stumbled sideways, one wing dragging.

Another Quick Attack. Pidgeot went down.

Koda recalled it gently.

*You did exactly what I asked. That's enough.*

Arcanine stepped forward without being called.

The gym went quiet.

Raichu and Arcanine regarded each other across the floor — two powerful Pokémon who understood immediately that this was the real battle.

Surge's eyes sharpened for the first time.

---

Raichu opened with Thunderbolt — full power, direct line.

"Arcanine — move!"

Arcanine launched sideways with Extreme Speed, the bolt scorching the floor where he'd stood. He circled immediately, forcing Raichu to track him.

*Keep it turning. Raichu's power is in straight lines.*

Raichu pivoted fast — another Thunderbolt cutting across the arc. Arcanine took a glancing hit, stumbled, kept moving.

*He's tiring. Can't keep this pace forever.*

Koda watched the floor. The scorched lines from Raichu's bolts. The pattern.

*It always fires from planted feet. Every time it stops to aim, there's a half second.*

"Arcanine — draw one more. Then straight in. Flamethrower at full."

Arcanine curved wide, deliberate, almost slow.

Raichu planted. Drew back.

*Now.*

"Flamethrower!"

Arcanine pivoted on a single stride and drove straight forward — Flamethrower launching point blank before Raichu could release. The heat hit Raichu square, interrupting the Thunderbolt mid-charge.

Raichu skidded back hard. Stayed up.

Barely.

It looked at Arcanine with something that wasn't quite anger. More like recognition.

Arcanine stood firm, breathing hard, fur singed along one side.

Koda felt the air around him — instinct reading the pressure, the heat, the charged static still crackling across the floor. His fingers moved slightly at his side.

*No.*

*This is Arcanine's moment. Not mine.*

"One more," Koda said quietly. "You've got it. Extreme Speed."

Arcanine pulled everything left into a single burst — closing the distance in a heartbeat, the impact clean and decisive.

Raichu sat down slowly.

The gym was silent.

---

Surge recalled Raichu without a word. He stood still for a long moment, looking at Arcanine — then at Koda.

He crossed the floor in four strides and held out a hand.

Koda shook it.

"You didn't use your air once," Surge said. Quiet. Knowing.

Koda met his eyes and said nothing.

Surge studied him for another second. Then reached into his jacket and held out the Thunder Badge — small, gold, shaped like a lightning bolt.

"Your Arcanine read Raichu better than most experienced trainers do," he said. "Whatever you're doing — keep doing it."

Koda took the badge. Looked at it once. Then looked back at Arcanine.

Arcanine was already watching him — tired, steady, proud.

Koda walked to him and pressed a hand to his side.

"That was yours," he said. "All of it."

Arcanine exhaled slowly and leaned into him.

Three badges.

And Surge's final words stayed with Koda as he pushed through the gym's heavy steel doors and back into the Vermilion City afternoon —

*You didn't use your air once.*

He almost had. Twice.

But that was the whole point.
