# What They Built, Part Four

Athena appeared at the corner of the compound.

"Done," she said. Her voice was steady but her eyes were bright — the particular look she got when animals were in distress and she could feel all of it.

Koda released the wind column.

Golbat dropped ten feet, caught itself, and immediately fled south without looking back.

He looked at the agents.

"We're leaving," he said. "You're not going to follow us."

He said it the way Arcanine stood — not as a threat. As a fact.

The agents didn't move.

---

They cleared the compound perimeter at a controlled walk — fast enough to create distance, measured enough to stay ready.

A hundred meters into the scrubland Athena stopped and crouched, hands shaking slightly now that the immediate thing was over. Eevee pressed against her immediately.

She put both hands on Eevee and breathed.

"How many?" Koda asked.

"Twenty-three," she said. "All gone now."

He looked back at the compound — still and dark against the amber sky, its antenna arrays silent for the first time.

The interference in the air was already clearing. The natural current returning, finding its old paths through the scrubland like water returning to a riverbed.

Dragonair moved beside him and rested its head against his shoulder — something it had never done standing up before.

He put a hand on it.

*I know. That was hard. You held when it mattered.*

Arcanine pressed to his other side.

All of them tired. All of them whole.

Athena stood and looked at the clearing air with something complicated on her face.

"Sabrina knew," she said.

"She knew," Koda agreed.

"And she waited for someone to go look."

"She needed someone who would handle it the right way." He looked at her. "Not just power through it."

Athena was quiet for a moment.

Then she looked at the scrubland — already, somewhere in the distance, the sound of wildlife returning. Cautious. Testing. Coming back to a place that had stopped being wrong.

"Okay," she said softly.

Just that. Okay.

They turned west toward Saffron City and walked back through the clearing air as the last of the evening light went gold and then dark around them.
