# The Road Between Storms and Stone, Part Three

## Battle Three — Morning Ambush

They were barely an hour back on the trail when four trainers stepped out from both sides — a planned double ambush, grinning like they'd been waiting all morning.

"Heard you two are good," the lead one said. "Prove it. Two on two, simultaneous."

Four battles at once — two per side.

*Chaotic,* Koda thought. *Good practice.*

He looked at Meadow.

She looked back.

No words needed.

**Koda — Pidgeot vs. Fearow**

A sky battle. Pure speed, pure instinct. Fearow was experienced — drilling fast, changing angles mid-dive.

Koda watched the airflow. *There.* A slight pressure difference where Fearow committed too hard on a left bank.

"Pidgeot — wait for the overcorrection. Then Steel Wing."

Pidgeot held, absorbed a Peck hit without breaking, then launched off the impact's momentum — Steel Wing catching Fearow completely off-guard.

One clean hit. Fearow down.

**Meadow — Bulbasaur vs. Sandshrew**

Sandshrew dug immediately, vanishing underground.

Meadow crouched. Fingers to the soil.

She tracked it.

"Left of the second bush," she said quietly. "Now."

Bulbasaur drove vines directly into the ground at that exact point. Sandshrew burst back up, disoriented, and a follow-up Tackle ended it.

The ambush trainers stared.

"How did she know where it was?" one whispered.

Koda smiled and kept walking.

---

By the time Vermilion City's gate came into view that afternoon, their Pokémon had stopped separating by trainer. Arcanine walked next to Bulbasaur. Geodude rolled alongside Kadabra. Pidgeot circled wide enough to watch all of them.

"They're becoming one team," Meadow observed.

"Yeah." Koda watched Arcanine nudge Bulbasaur gently with his nose. "They are."

She adjusted the strap on her bag.

"I'm not going for the Vermilion badge," she said. "My path goes a different direction for a while."

Koda nodded. He'd half expected it.

"Then we split here."

"For now," she said. Not a goodbye. A *see you later* wrapped in two words.

Bulbasaur looked up at Arcanine for a long moment. Then trotted back to Meadow's side.

Arcanine watched it go, then pressed his head briefly against Koda's shoulder.

Koda scratched behind his ears.

"I know," he said. "Me too."

He watched Meadow take the eastern fork without looking back — steady, unhurried, exactly like herself — until the tree line swallowed her up.

Then he turned toward Vermilion City.

Third badge waiting.
