# What They Built, Part One

The structure appeared through the scrubland like something that had decided the landscape didn't matter — a low sprawling compound, prefabricated walls, antenna arrays along the roof sending interference patterns into the air that Koda could feel like static against his skin.

Three guards outside. Two Pokémon patrols visible. Cages along the eastern wall — a long row, occupied.

Koda and Athena were flat in the dry grass thirty meters out, watching.

"Twelve cages minimum," Athena murmured. "All wild Pokémon. I can feel them — they're exhausted. Whatever they're doing in there has been draining them."

Koda looked at the antenna arrays.

*They're using the Pokémon as a power source,* he realized. *The interference in the air — it's being generated by forced energy output. They're taking it from them.*

His jaw tightened.

"How do you want to do this?" Athena asked.

He watched the patrol pattern. Thirty second rotation. A gap on the western wall every forty-five seconds when both patrols faced east simultaneously.

"Fast," he said. "We go through the western gap. You get to the cages. I handle what comes at us."

"And if a lot comes at us?"

He looked at her.

"Then I handle a lot."

---

They moved on the next gap.

Arcanine and Dragonair with Koda. Eevee and Pidgeotto with Athena. Through the western gap clean — no alarm, no shout.

Then a guard turned early.

"HEY—"

Everything happened at once.

Two Rocket agents released Pokémon simultaneously — Arbok and Weezing, the standard pair, moving with practiced coordination. Three more agents came around the eastern corner at a run, releasing as they moved. Electrode. Golbat. Victreebel.

Five Pokémon. Two trainers.

*Fast. Don't let them set up.*

"Athena — cages. Go. Don't stop."

"Koda—"

"Go!"

She went.
