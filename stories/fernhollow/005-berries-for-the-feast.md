# Berries for the Feast

The Fernhollow Feast was tomorrow!

"Every dinosaur brings something to share," said Ranger Juno. "We'll bring berries."

She gave each boy a basket. Toot came too. *Toot, toot!*

The berry bushes were heavy with fat purple berries. {{hero}} picked. {{brother}} picked. *Plop, plop, plop.*

Toot didn't pick. Toot ate.

*Munch, munch, munch.*

"Toot!" said {{brother}}. "Those are for the feast!"

*Toot,* said Toot, with purple juice all over his nose.

They filled the baskets to the top and set them in the shade. Then they went to wash their hands in the river.

When they came back, the baskets were empty.

And Toot was lying on his back, with a very round tummy.

*Tooooo,* he groaned.

"Oh, Toot," said {{hero}}. "You ate them *all*?"

"He has a tummy ache," said Juno. "That's what happens when you eat everybody's share."

{{brother}} was mad. But Toot looked so sorry. He covered his eyes with his little hands.

{{brother}} rubbed Toot's tummy, round and round.

"It's okay," he said. "But now there's nothing for the feast."

Toot rolled onto his feet. He sniffed the air. Then he trotted off through the ferns, tooting for them to follow.

Behind the big rocks was a secret patch of bushes. They were *covered* in berries. More than before!

"Toot! You found more!"

This time, Toot didn't eat a single one. He held the basket in his mouth while the boys picked.

Well. He ate *one*.

"One is fair," said {{brother}}.
