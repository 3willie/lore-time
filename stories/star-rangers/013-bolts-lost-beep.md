# Bolt's Lost Beep

One morning, Bolt opened his mouth to say good morning.

Nothing came out.

He tried again. Not a word. Not even a *beep*.

"Oh no," said {{hero}}. "Bolt's voice box is broken!"

"*It needs a new part,*" said {{mom}} on the radio. "*It'll come on the next supply ship. Three days.*"

Three days without talking!

Bolt's eyes drooped. He looked so sad.

"How will we know what he wants?" asked {{brother}}.

"Let's watch," said {{hero}}. "Like on the Quiet Planet."

So they watched.

When Bolt was happy, his eyes went round and bright.

When he was worried, his eyes blinked fast. *Blink-blink-blink.*

When he was hungry for power, he patted the plug on his tummy.

When he wanted a hug, he held out his long skinny arms.

"He's talking all the time," said {{brother}}. "Just not with words!"

They made up hand signs together. A thumbs up meant *yes*. A wiggle meant *no*. A tap on the head meant *I'm thinking*.

At lunch, Bolt held up three fingers and pointed at the crackers.

"Three crackers?" said {{brother}}.

Bolt wiggled. *No.* He pointed at the boys. Then himself. Then three fingers.

"Oh! One cracker for each of us!" said {{hero}}.

Thumbs up!

By the third day, they understood almost everything.

Then the new part came. *Click.*

"Hello!" said Bolt. "Oh, it's nice to talk again. But do you know what?"

"What?" asked {{brother}}.

Bolt held out his arms.

The boys knew exactly what that meant.
