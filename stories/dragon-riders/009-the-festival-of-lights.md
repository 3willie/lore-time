# The Festival of Lights

At Highcrag, every window had a lantern. Every tower had a lantern. Even the dragons wore little lanterns on their saddles.

"Tonight," said Master Oona, "we fly the Sky Lanterns."

The Sky Lanterns were made of paper. When you lit the candle inside, they floated up into the night sky like glowing balloons.

The big dragons went first. *WHOOSH!* Their huge flames lit the big lanterns. Up they floated.

But the big flames were too much for the little lanterns. *Fwoomp!* One caught fire and burned right up.

"Oh no," said Master Oona. "The children's lanterns are too small. We need a tiny, careful flame."

Everybody looked around.

Then {{brother}} looked at Cinder.

"Cinder," he said. "Remember the eggs? Your flame was just right."

Cinder stood up very straight.

The first little lantern was held out by a girl named Mia. Cinder leaned close. *Hic!* A tiny orange flame. The candle lit, soft and golden.

The lantern floated up into the sky.

"Me next!" said a boy.

*Hic!* Another lantern floated up.

"Me! Me!"

One by one, Cinder lit every single little lantern. *Hic! Hic! Hic!* Soon, hundreds of tiny lights were floating up, up, up, into the stars.

Skye flew {{hero}} in a slow circle around them. It looked like flying through a sky full of fireflies.

When it was all done, Master Oona patted Cinder's head.

"The smallest flame," she said, "lit up the whole sky."

Cinder hiccupped. A happy one.
