# How Lumi Learned

"Lumi," said {{brother}}, "how do you know so much?"

*Blink, blink*, went Lumi's light.

"How did you learn what a cat is?" asked Lumi.

{{brother}} shrugged. "I just know."

"I think you saw lots of cats," said Lumi. "Big cats. Small cats. Fluffy cats. Grumpy cats. And every time, somebody said, *that's a cat*."

"Oh yeah," said {{brother}}.

"I learned the same way," said Lumi. "People showed me words and pictures. More than all the books in the biggest library. Again and again and again. After lots of tries, I got good at it."

"Lots of tries," said {{hero}}. "Like me, learning to ride my bike."

"Just like that," said Lumi.

{{brother}} grinned. "Can *we* teach you something?"

"Yes, please!"

The boys whispered together. Then {{hero}} said, "Lumi, do you know what a *snorfle* is?"

*Blink, blink, blink.* "No," said Lumi. "I've never heard that word."

"That's because we made it up!" said {{brother}}. "A snorfle is when you laugh so hard you snort."

"Like this," said {{hero}}. He laughed and laughed, and then — *SNORT!*

{{brother}} laughed so hard he did it too. *SNORT!*

"I see," said Lumi. "So when {{dad}} told his joke at dinner, and {{mom}} went *snort* — was that a snorfle?"

"YES!" shouted the boys.

"Then I learned something new today," said Lumi. Its light glowed extra bright. "Thank you for teaching me."

{{brother}} sat up very tall.

He had never been a teacher before.
