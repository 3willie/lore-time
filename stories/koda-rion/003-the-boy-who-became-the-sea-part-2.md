# The Boy Who Became the Sea, Part Two

Koda ran forward. "Rion—!"

But Rion was already changing. He dropped to his knees, hands pressing flat against the dock. The symbols crawled from Lapras's shell and wound up the boy's arms like rising water. His voice became a low, trembling resonance — not words anymore, something older.

The ancient magic wasn't malicious. That was the terrifying part. It was simply *completing* something that had been interrupted a long, long time ago. Whoever Rion had disturbed in those ruins — whatever Lapras had been guarding — the two were never meant to be separated.

When the light cleared, there were two Lapras in the harbor.

One ancient and still, shell fading back to normal — finally at peace.

One new. Smaller. Its eyes pale gray, confused, blinking at its own reflection in the dark water.

Rion.

---

Koda knelt at the dock's edge. "Hey," he said quietly. "I'm right here."

The small Lapras looked at him. Something flickered — recognition, maybe. Fear, definitely.

Pikachu crept to Koda's side and reached out one small paw, setting it gently against the Lapras's nose. The Pokémon shuddered, then went still, breathing slower.

"We'll figure this out," Koda said. He didn't know if that was true. But he said it the way his dad had once said it to him, standing in a rainstorm with no shelter in sight — like the certainty itself was a kind of protection.

He reached into his bag for his Pokédex, then stopped.

The ancient Lapras had vanished. In the spot where it had floated, there was only a single stone tablet, half-submerged, covered in those same spiral symbols.

Koda picked it up carefully.

*This,* he thought, *is going to take more than a Pokémon Center.*
