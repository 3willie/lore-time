# The Last Night of Summer

Summer was almost over.

"Tomorrow we go home," said {{dad}}.

{{brother}}'s lip wobbled. "I don't want to go. Who will sit with the blue lamp? Who will give Ember cookies?"

"Nell and I will," said Old Wick. "We're a team again, thanks to you."

But {{brother}} was still sad. {{hero}} was too.

That night, they climbed the stairs one more time. Round and round and round.

They said goodnight to the green lamp. Goodnight to the blue lamp. Goodnight to the silver lamp, and the little red lamp.

At the top, the Great Lamp was shining gold.

"I have something for you," said Old Wick.

He reached into his biggest pocket. Out came two tiny lanterns, each one no bigger than an acorn, on a string.

"Pocket lanterns," he said. "They don't need patience, or honesty, or remembering. They only need one thing."

"What?" asked {{hero}}.

"Think of Gull Rock."

{{brother}} closed his eyes. He thought of the birds, and the seal pup, and Ember's glowing tail.

His little lantern lit up. *Twinkle!*

So did {{hero}}'s.

"Whenever you miss us," said Nell, "there we'll be."

In the morning, the little boat went *putt-putt-putt* across the sea. Old Wick and Nell waved from the dock. Ember waved his tail.

"Goodbye!" called the boys.

"Not goodbye," called Old Wick. "*See you next summer*, keepers!"

Gull Rock got smaller and smaller.

But in two pockets, two tiny lanterns kept glowing.

All the way home.
