# Pickles and Peace

The second tablet was smaller than the first.

Koda found it wedged under a mossy rock beside a bubbling hot spring, three days into his search for answers. He picked it up, squinted at the spiral symbols, and made the critical error of holding it too close to his face.

The light was instantaneous.

---

When it cleared, Koda was approximately ten inches tall.

He looked down at his tiny yellow paws. He looked at his reflection in the spring. Round cheeks. Long ears. Little bolt-shaped tail flickering with nervous electricity.

"Oh," said Koda.

"Pika," said Koda.

He sat down in the grass and took a very long breath.

Rion — small Lapras, floating in the hot spring with his fins resting on the edge like armrests — opened one gray eye and stared at him.

Then, for the first time since the harbor, Rion laughed. It came out as a long, warbling Lapras trill, but it was unmistakably a laugh.

Koda pointed one tiny paw at him. His cheeks sparked.

---

They never did find a cure that week.

What they found instead was a shallow pool fed by the hot spring, perfectly warm, perfectly quiet, tucked between two boulders with a view of the afternoon sky. Rion fit in it just right. Koda sat on the edge with his feet dangling in.

Athena visited on the third day, took one long look at both of them, pressed her lips together very hard, and produced two cucumber slices from her bag without a word.

She placed one over each of Rion's eyes.

She looked at Koda.

Koda held out his tiny paw.

She placed one cucumber slice over each of his little Pikachu eyes and sat down on a rock nearby, opening her book with the practiced calm of someone who had simply decided this was her life now.

The sun was warm. The spring burbled softly. Somewhere distant, a Pidgey called.

"Pika," said Koda contentedly.

Rion let out a long, slow breath that rippled the water around him.

Neither of them moved for a very long time.

---

*Some quests, it turns out, could wait until Monday.*
