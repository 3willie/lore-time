# Smoke in the Trees, Part One

Koda smelled it before he saw it.

Not fire — something chemical. Sharp and artificial, the kind of smell that didn't belong in a forest. It moved against the wind, which meant it was coming from multiple directions at once.

He stopped walking.

The forest had gone quiet. No Pidgey calls. No rustling in the undergrowth. The particular silence that meant every wild Pokémon in the area had already made a decision and gone still.

*Something's wrong.*

He recalled Pidgeot from the air quietly — didn't want eyes on them from above. Kept Arcanine close. Let Kadabra drift forward slightly, scanning.

Kadabra's eyes flickered. It looked back at Koda and tilted its head left.

*That direction.*

---

He moved through the trees carefully, keeping low, reading the airflow as he went. The pressure pattern ahead was wrong — too many moving bodies in a concentrated space, the air pushed and displaced in ways that mapped out a rough picture before he saw anything.

*At least six people. Machinery. Something large being dragged.*

He crouched at the tree line and looked through.

A wide forest clearing — and Team Rocket had turned it into an operation.

Four agents in black uniforms worked a net system stretched between the trees — thick cables connected to a central winch, designed to drop and contain anything that moved through the space. Two more agents managed a set of cages along the far edge, already holding three Pokémon. A Nidoran. Two Bellsprout. All pressed to the back of their cages, frightened and still.

A seventh figure stood in the center directing everything — older than the others, sharp-eyed, a clipboard in hand like this was inventory work.

*They're running a harvest,* Koda realized. *Systematic. They've done this before.*

He looked at his team. Kept his voice barely above a breath.

"We take down the net system first. Free what's in the cages. Then we deal with the agents."

Arcanine's ears were flat and forward. Ready.

Kadabra's eyes were already cold.

Dratini had gone completely still in a way Koda recognized now — not fear. Focused. The way it looked before Dragon Rage.

Pidgeot's ball was in his hand.

*All of us.*

---

## The Net System

Koda sent Kadabra forward through the trees on the left flank — silent, floating low, invisible against the shadow.

Two agents near the winch heard nothing before Confusion lifted them both off their feet and set them firmly but harmlessly against a tree trunk, where they stayed, dazed and pinned.

The winch operator turned and shouted.

That was the signal.

"Arcanine — Flamethrower, cut the net cables!"

Arcanine burst from the tree line, low and fast, sweeping a controlled line of fire across the cable rigging. The synthetic lines caught immediately — engineered to be tough but not fireproof. The net system collapsed inward, useless.

Two agents came at Arcanine directly with their own Pokémon — an Ekans and a Koffing.

*There it is.*

"Arcanine — yours. Pidgeot —"

He released Pidgeot upward into the canopy.

"— keep Koffing away from the cages. If that thing uses Smog near the captured Pokémon—"

Pidgeot was already moving, cutting between Koffing and the cage line with a Steel Wing that sent Koffing spinning wide. It repositioned immediately, blocking the angle again.

*Good. Hold that space.*
