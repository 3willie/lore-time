# The Boy Who Became the Sea

The port town of Cerulean Cove had a rule: no battles on the docks. Rion had never followed it once.

He was waiting when Koda arrived — leaning against a wooden post with his arms crossed, Lapras floating lazily behind him in the harbor. Rion was maybe a year older than Koda, with salt-bleached hair and cold eyes that didn't smile even when his mouth did.

"Heard you beat a Gigantamax Lapras bare-handed," Rion said. "Figured that was my cue."

Koda looked at the Lapras in the water. Something was wrong with it. Its shell was etched with markings he'd never seen before — spiraling symbols that pulsed faintly blue, like veins of light just beneath the surface.

"Those markings," Koda said carefully. "Where did you find it?"

Rion shrugged. "Old ruins. Past the reef. Lapras was sleeping inside." He smiled. "Finders keepers."

Koda didn't like that answer. But Rion was already calling the battle.

---

"Lapras, **Blizzard!**"

The harbor exploded into white. Koda ducked behind a dock pylon, pulling Pikachu close. When the freeze passed, he straightened.

"Pikachu — **Quick Attack**, keep moving!"

Pikachu was a golden streak across the water's surface, feet barely touching each wave before launching to the next. Lapras tracked it with those glowing eyes, the symbols on its shell burning brighter with every move.

*They're reacting,* Koda realized. *The markings are feeding off the battle.*

"Pikachu, break off — **Thunderbolt, wide arc!**"

The bolt spread across the water in a crackling curtain. It struck Lapras clean, and the Pokémon cried out — but the cry was strange. Long. Mournful. Like something waking up that had been asleep for centuries.

The markings blazed.

A column of blue-white light erupted from the harbor floor and swallowed Lapras whole. Rion stumbled backward, shielding his eyes.

"What—"

The light bent sideways.

And wrapped around *him.*

---

Koda ran forward. "Rion—!"

But Rion was already changing. He dropped to his knees, hands pressing flat against the dock. The symbols crawled from Lapras's shell and wound up the boy's arms like rising water. His voice became a low, trembling resonance — not words anymore, something older.

The ancient magic wasn't malicious. That was the terrifying part. It was simply *completing* something that had been interrupted a long, long time ago. Whoever Rion had disturbed in those ruins — whatever Lapras had been guarding — the two were never meant to be separated.

When the light cleared, there were two Lapras in the harbor.

One ancient and still, shell fading back to normal — finally at peace.

One new. Smaller. Its eyes pale gray, confused, blinking at its own reflection in the dark water.

Rion.

---

Koda knelt at the dock's edge. "Hey," he said quietly. "I'm right here."

The small Lapras looked at him. Something flickered — recognition, maybe. Fear, definitely.

Pikachu crept to Koda's side and reached out one small paw, setting it gently against the Lapras's nose. The Pokémon shuddered, then went still, breathing slower.

"We'll figure this out," Koda said. He didn't know if that was true. But he said it the way his dad had once said it to him, standing in a rainstorm with no shelter in sight — like the certainty itself was a kind of protection.

He reached into his bag for his Pokédex, then stopped.

The ancient Lapras had vanished. In the spot where it had floated, there was only a single stone tablet, half-submerged, covered in those same spiral symbols.

Koda picked it up carefully.

*This,* he thought, *is going to take more than a Pokémon Center.*
