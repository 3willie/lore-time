# A New Kind of Helper

{{mom}} came home with a box.

"What is it?" asked {{brother}}. "Is it a toy? Is it a puppy?"

"Open it and see," said {{mom}}.

Inside was a small, round thing, like a smooth white pebble. {{mom}} set it on the kitchen table and tapped the top.

*Bing!* It began to glow, soft and yellow.

"Hello," said a friendly voice. "I'm Lumi. What are your names?"

The boys stared.

"I'm {{hero}}," said {{hero}}.

"I'm {{brother}}," said {{brother}}. "Are you a person?"

"No," said Lumi. "I'm not a person."

"Are you a pet?"

"No. I don't eat, and I don't have fur."

"Are you a toy?"

"Not quite," said Lumi. "I'm a helper. People made me. They taught me lots and lots of words, so I can answer questions and help with ideas."

{{hero}} thought about that. "So you're a new kind of thing?"

Lumi's light went *blink, blink*. That's what it did when it was thinking.

"Yes," said Lumi. "I think that's just right. A new kind of thing."

"What can you do?" asked {{brother}}.

"Ask me something," said Lumi.

{{brother}} thought hard. "How tall is a giraffe?"

*Blink, blink.* "As tall as three grown-ups standing on each other's shoulders," said Lumi.

"WOW," said {{brother}}.

"Can you get me a cookie?" asked {{hero}}.

"No," said Lumi. "I don't have any hands."

Everybody laughed. Even {{mom}}.

"So Lumi knows about giraffes," said {{hero}}, "but can't get cookies."

"That's right," said {{mom}}. "A helper helps. But we still do lots of things ourselves."

And she got everyone a cookie.
