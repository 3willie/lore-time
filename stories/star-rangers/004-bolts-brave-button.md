# Bolt's Brave Button

The asteroid ring looked like a river of floating rocks, tumbling slowly around a dark planet. The map said the last piece was somewhere inside.

"I do not like this," said Bolt. "I would like to say that very clearly. I do not like this at all."

The *Pebble* nosed carefully between the rocks. Left. Right. Up. Then — *clunk*. A big rock bumped them, and the ship lurched to a stop.

"We're stuck," {{hero}} said. "Something's caught on the outside."

{{mom}}'s voice crackled over the speaker. "Rangers, you'll need to unhook it by hand. Someone has to go out on a spacewalk."

{{hero}} and {{brother}} looked at each other. They were too big for the little side hatch.

Slowly, they both turned to look at Bolt.

Bolt's eyes blinked very, very fast. "Oh no," he said. "Oh no, no. I'm the worrying one. You know I'm the worrying one."

"You don't have to go," {{hero}} said. "We'll think of something else."

Bolt was quiet for a long time. Then he opened a little panel on his chest. Inside was a small red button with a word painted on it: *BRAVE*.

"My builder gave me this," he said. "She said if I ever needed to be brave, I should press it. But I never have. What if I press it and nothing happens? What if I'm still scared?"

{{brother}} thought about the dark, and thunderstorms, and the first day of school.

"I think brave isn't *not* scared," he said. "I think brave is being scared and doing the thing anyway."

Bolt looked at him. Then he closed the panel without pressing the button.

"Then I'll do it the real way," he said.

He clipped on his safety line, took a deep wobbly breath (robots don't need to breathe, but it seemed to help), and climbed out into space.

The boys pressed their faces to the window. They watched Bolt inch along the hull, shaking like a leaf, until he reached the jammed rock — and gave it one big shove.

The *Pebble* floated free.

When Bolt climbed back inside, he was holding something. A torn scrap of glowing blue paper.

The fourth piece. With all four together, the map finally showed it: a path past the Nebula, straight to a star that burned silver and gold.

"I'm still scared," Bolt admitted.

"We know," said {{hero}}, hugging him. "That's what made it brave."
