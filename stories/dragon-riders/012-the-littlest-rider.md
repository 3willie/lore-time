# The Littlest Rider

It was Choosing Day again.

The new kids stood in a line in the courtyard. They looked nervous. One little boy at the end looked *very* nervous. He was the smallest one there.

{{brother}} watched him.

"That was me," he whispered to {{hero}}. "Last year."

The little boy's name was Finn. His hands were shaking.

"What if nobody chooses me?" said Finn.

{{brother}} walked over.

"Somebody will," he said. "Just be yourself. That's what Master Oona says."

"But I'm so small."

{{brother}} smiled. "So was I. And Cinder fell in the water trough."

Finn giggled. Just a little.

Then — *WHOOSH!* — the sky filled with wings. The young dragons came down from the mountains.

One by one, they chose their riders. Finn watched. He waited. Nobody came near him.

His lip began to wobble.

Then, from behind a rock, a tiny dragon peeked out. She was gray and fluffy, like a cloud. She was even smaller than Cinder.

She was too shy to come out.

"Look," whispered {{brother}}. "She's scared too."

Finn looked. Then he did something very brave. He sat down on the ground. He kept still. He held out his hand. Not grabbing. Just offering.

The little gray dragon crept closer. And closer. And then she pressed her nose against his hand.

"*Chosen!*" called Master Oona.

Finn's face lit up like a Sky Lantern.

"Her name is Puff!" he shouted.

{{brother}} cheered louder than anybody.

"You taught him the trick," said {{hero}}.

"Someone taught me," said {{brother}}.
