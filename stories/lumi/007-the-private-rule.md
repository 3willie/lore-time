# The Private Rule

{{brother}} was telling Lumi everything.

"My favorite color is green. My favorite food is noodles. I have a wiggly tooth!"

"How exciting," said Lumi.

"And guess what?" said {{brother}}. "I know where we hide our spare house key. It's under the—"

"Wait!" said {{mom}}.

{{brother}} stopped. His mouth was still open.

{{mom}} sat down beside him. "You're not in trouble," she said. "But some things are *private*. That means they're just for our family."

"Like what?" asked {{hero}}.

"Like where our key is hidden. And where we live. And our secret passwords."

"But Lumi is nice," said {{brother}}.

*Blink, blink.* "Thank you," said Lumi. "But your mom is right. It's a good rule for every helper, and every screen, and anyone you don't know well."

"What's the rule?" asked {{brother}}.

"Before you share something private," said {{mom}}, "ask a grown-up first."

{{hero}} wanted to practice. "Is my favorite color private?"

"No," said {{mom}}. "You can tell anyone that."

"Is my wiggly tooth private?" asked {{brother}}.

"No. Wiggle away."

"Is our address private?"

"Yes," said {{mom}}. "Ask first."

"Is the hiding place for the key private?"

"Very," said {{mom}}.

{{brother}} nodded slowly. Then he turned to Lumi.

"Lumi, I'm not going to tell you where the key is."

"Good," said Lumi. "I don't need to know."

"But I *will* tell you about my tooth."

"Yes, please," said Lumi. "Is it very wiggly?"

"SO wiggly," said {{brother}}. And he showed everybody.
