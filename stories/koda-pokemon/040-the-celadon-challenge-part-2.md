# The Celadon Challenge, Part Two

## Koda vs. Erika

She opened with Tangela.

Koda sent out Pidgeot.

Tangela's vines moved constantly — a living disruption field that made clean approaches difficult. Every time Pidgeot committed to a dive, vines redirected or wrapped, turning speed into a liability.

*It's not fighting back hard. It's just making everything difficult.*

"Pidgeot — don't dive. Work the air. Gust, wide and sustained."

Pidgeot shifted strategy — staying high, beating wings in steady controlled bursts that pushed Tangela's vines back, disrupting its field without entering it. Tangela struggled to maintain reach against the constant pressure.

"Steel Wing — straight down, fast exit."

Pidgeot folded, connected cleanly, and climbed before the vines could respond.

Tangela sat down.

Erika nodded slightly. "You adjusted quickly."

"It took a moment too long," Koda replied honestly.

She almost smiled. Released her second Pokémon.

Weepinbell.

It moved faster than Tangela — aggressive in a way the first hadn't been, launching Razor Leaf immediately in a wide spread that forced Pidgeot into a defensive climb.

Koda called Pidgeot back.

"Arcanine."

Weepinbell's Razor Leaf scattered off Arcanine's thick coat harmlessly. Arcanine read the distance and launched a controlled Flamethrower — precise, not excessive, conscious of the gym walls.

Weepinbell went down cleanly.

One left.

Erika reached for her third ball with the same unhurried calm she'd had throughout.

Vileplume emerged onto the platform — fully evolved, heavy with pollen, the air around it immediately thickening with a yellow-green haze.

Koda felt it. Sleep Powder and Stun Spore mixed in the air around Vileplume like a passive weapon. The longer a Pokémon stayed near it, the worse the situation became.

*This one needs to end fast.*

"Arcanine — Extreme Speed, Flamethrower point blank. Don't let it settle."

Arcanine launched forward — but Vileplume released a Stun Spore burst on contact and Arcanine staggered, electricity crawling through his legs, slowing him mid-stride.

The follow-up Petal Dance hit hard. Arcanine went down.

Koda recalled him steadily.

*Okay. That's what she was saving it for.*

He looked at his remaining Pokémon.

Kadabra was the logical choice. Psychic type, range, no need to get close.

He reached for Dratini's ball instead.
