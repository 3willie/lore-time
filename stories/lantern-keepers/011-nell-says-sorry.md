# Nell Says Sorry

Something was wrong with Old Wick.

He was polishing the lamps too hard. *Rub, rub, rub.* He didn't hum. He didn't wink.

"Are you grumpy?" asked {{brother}}.

"No," said Old Wick, in a grumpy voice.

At lunch, Nell said, "Wick, you polish those lamps just right."

"Somebody had to," said Old Wick. "All those years."

Then it was very quiet.

{{hero}} whispered to his brother. "I think he's sad that Nell went away for so long."

{{brother}} knew what to do about sad. He fetched the little red lamp and put it in the middle of the table.

"This one lights for kindness," he said. "Maybe it can help."

Nell looked at the lamp. Then she looked at her old friend.

"Wick," she said. "When Ada moved away, I was too sad to stay. So I left too. And I left you with all the work." She took a breath. "I'm sorry."

Old Wick's beard wobbled.

"I missed you," he said. "That's all. I missed my friend."

"I missed you too," said Nell.

On the table, the little red lamp began to glow.

Old Wick laughed. He wiped his eyes with a polishing cloth, which left a smudge on his nose.

"Sorry is a kind of kindness," said {{hero}}.

"The hardest kind," said Nell. "And the best."

Old Wick poured everybody tea. The boys had theirs with lots of milk.

And that afternoon, two old friends polished the lamps together.

Old Wick hummed the whole time.
