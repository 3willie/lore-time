# The Sky Lights

It was the Rangers' last night on Snow Planet.

"Come outside," said Mrs. Flap. "We have something to show you."

Everybody lay down on their backs in the snow. The penguins. The boys. Bolt.

The sky was dark.

Then — *ooooh!*

A ribbon of green light rippled across the sky. Then a pink one. Then purple. They swirled and danced, like scarves in the wind.

"The sky lights," whispered Pip. "They come when the year has been a good one."

"I count four colors," said Bolt. "No, five. No… I'll stop counting. I'll just look."

Far above the dancing lights, one star twinkled silver and gold.

"That's our Hidden Star!" said {{brother}}. "It's saying hello!"

They watched for a long, long time.

"Thank you for teaching us to slide," said {{brother}}.

"Thank you for the Huddle," said {{hero}}.

"Thank you for sliding slowly with me," said Pip to Bolt.

Bolt's eyes glowed. "Thank you for letting me be scared," he said. "And for letting me try anyway."

Mrs. Flap gave each Ranger a tiny scarf. Bolt's was red.

In the morning, the *Pebble* lifted off. *Whoosh!* All the penguins waved their flippers.

"*Rangers,*" said {{mom}} on the radio. "*How was Snow Planet?*"

"Cold," said {{hero}}.

"Fast," said {{brother}}.

"Cozy," said Bolt, in his red scarf.

"*I want to hear everything,*" said {{mom}}. "*Come home. I'm making hot cocoa.*"

And the little ship flew home, under a sky full of stars.
