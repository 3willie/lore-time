# Theo's First Place

Baby Theo wasn't a baby anymore. He could walk now. Wobbly, but walking.

Aunt Rosa brought him over to play. And she brought his map too.

"It drew something!" she said. "I think it was when he shared his banana with the dog."

The boys looked. On Theo's map, next to his little house, there was a new drawing. A sandbox.

*Theo's Sandbox*, said the tiny letters.

"His very first place!" said {{brother}}.

"Let's take him to play in a real sandbox," said {{hero}}.

At the park, Theo sat in the sand. He picked some up. He tried to eat it.

"No, Theo, sand is yucky," laughed {{brother}}.

{{brother}} wanted to build a big castle. A huge one, with towers.

But every time he built a tower — *SPLAT!* — Theo knocked it down. And laughed.

"Theo!" said {{brother}}. "Stop it!"

Theo's lip wobbled.

{{hero}} sat down next to them.

"He's not being mean," said {{hero}}. "He's little. Knocking down is his favorite game."

{{brother}} remembered being little. When {{hero}} built things and {{brother}} wanted to help but didn't know how.

"Theo," said {{brother}}, "you can knock down *this* one. It's your special tower."

He built one small tower, just for Theo.

*SPLAT!* Theo squealed with joy.

So {{brother}} built more. Theo knocked them down. Over and over. Theo laughed so hard he fell over.

And while Theo was busy, {{hero}} and {{brother}} built their big castle on the other side.

On the way home, Theo fell asleep, holding {{brother}}'s finger.

"I think you'll be a good big cousin," said Aunt Rosa.
