# The Celadon Challenge, Part Three

## Dratini's Moment

Erika looked at the Poké Ball in Koda's hand.

"Dratini," she said. Not surprised. Curious.

"It needs this," Koda said simply.

Dratini emerged onto the platform and immediately felt the thickness of Vileplume's pollen cloud. Its eyes narrowed. It didn't retreat.

*Good,* Koda thought. *You feel it and you're staying anyway.*

Vileplume opened with Petal Dance — a sweeping storm of glowing petals that hit harder than it looked. Dratini took the first wave and skidded back a full foot.

It looked at Koda.

"Stay with it," he said quietly. "You've been in harder spots than this."

Dratini turned back.

Thunder Wave — it released the static arc low and fast, skimming the platform surface. Vileplume couldn't avoid the ground-level approach. The paralysis crawled up through its roots and slowed the Petal Dance rotation significantly.

*There. That's the window.*

"Wrap — get in close and hold."

Dratini moved forward into the pollen cloud without hesitating. Koda felt it — the effort it took, the burning in Dratini's eyes from the spores, the sheer decision to keep moving anyway.

It coiled around Vileplume and held.

Vileplume struggled — Sleep Powder releasing in close proximity, catching Dratini partially. Its movements slowed. Became heavy.

*It's going to go down if this doesn't end.*

"Dragon Rage," Koda said. "Everything you have."

Dratini pulled something up from deep — the blue-white energy building fast, building past what Koda had seen from it before, the glow intensifying until the whole platform was lit with it.

It released.

The burst hit Vileplume at point blank range and the shockwave pushed outward across the platform, scattering pollen in every direction, clearing the air completely.

Vileplume went down.

Dratini stood in the center of the cleared platform — swaying, eyes heavy with the Sleep Powder's edge, but standing.

Then something happened.
