# The Pocket Lantern

It was the first day back at school. {{brother}} was going to a brand-new class.

He stood at the door. There were so many kids. So much noise.

His tummy went *flip, flop*.

"I don't want to go in," he whispered.

{{hero}} knelt down. "Have you got your pocket lantern?"

{{brother}} reached into his pocket. There it was. The tiny lantern from Old Wick, no bigger than an acorn.

"Think of Gull Rock," said {{hero}}.

{{brother}} closed his eyes.

He thought of the blue lamp, and sitting very still. *In… and out.*

He thought of Ember's warm, glowing tail.

He thought of the waves going *swish, swish* on the rocks.

In his pocket, the little lantern went *twinkle*. He could feel it, warm in his hand.

His tummy stopped flipping. Well, mostly.

"Better?" asked {{hero}}.

"Better," said {{brother}}.

He walked into the classroom. The teacher smiled. "Hello! You can sit next to Ruby."

Ruby was holding her backpack very tight. Her eyes looked shiny, like she might cry.

{{brother}} knew that feeling.

"Do you want to hold this?" he whispered. He opened his hand to show her the lantern. "It helps when your tummy flips."

Ruby held it. "It's warm," she said.

"Think of somewhere happy," said {{brother}}.

Ruby closed her eyes. And the little lantern went *twinkle* for her too.

At pick-up time, {{brother}} ran out to {{hero}}.

"My class is good," he said. "And I made a friend. The lantern helped."

"*You* helped," said {{hero}}.
