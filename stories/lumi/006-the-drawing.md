# The Drawing

It was almost Grandma's birthday.

"I want to give her a picture of her dog, Biscuit," said {{hero}}. "But I can't draw dogs. They always look like potatoes."

Then he had an idea.

"Lumi! You make the picture. You'd do it perfectly."

*Blink, blink.*

"I could," said Lumi. "But then it would be *my* picture. Do you think Grandma wants a picture from me, or a picture from you?"

{{hero}} thought about it.

"From me," he said.

"I can help you learn," said Lumi. "Do you want a tip?"

"Yes, please."

"Start with a circle for the head. Then a bigger circle for the body."

{{hero}} drew a circle. It was wobbly. He drew a bigger circle. It was even wobblier.

"It looks like a snowman," he said.

"Keep going," said Lumi. "Add two floppy ears. Four legs. And a tail."

{{hero}} drew the ears. He drew the legs. He drew a long, waggy tail.

It wasn't perfect. One ear was bigger than the other. But it didn't look like a potato.

It looked like Biscuit!

{{hero}} colored him brown, with one white spot. At the bottom, he wrote his name, all by himself.

At the party, Grandma opened the picture. She put her hand on her heart.

"Did you draw this?" she asked.

"Lumi gave me tips," said {{hero}}. "But I did all the drawing."

"I can tell," said Grandma. "That's why I love it."

She put it on her fridge, right in the middle.

It's still there today.
