# Captain Rook's Birthday

"Guess what?" whispered Bolt. "It's Captain Rook's birthday tomorrow!"

"A party!" said {{brother}}. "Let's have a surprise party!"

They began to plan.

"We need a cake," said {{brother}}. "Chocolate. With sprinkles. And a dinosaur on top!"

"That's what *you* like," said {{hero}}. "What does Captain Rook like?"

{{brother}} thought. He didn't know.

"Let's find out," said {{hero}}. "Without spoiling the surprise."

So they went to visit the round, shiny pirate ship. They were very sneaky.

"Captain Rook," said {{hero}}, "what's your favorite food?"

"Seaweed pudding," she said. "Why?"

"No reason!"

"What's your favorite color?" asked {{brother}}.

"Purple, of course!"

Bolt looked around the ship. "I notice you have lots of drawings of fish."

"Oh, I love fish," said Captain Rook. "Especially the flying fish from the song."

The boys raced home with their ideas.

They made seaweed pudding. (It was very green and very wobbly.) They hung purple streamers everywhere. And {{brother}} drew a picture of a flying fish, with sparkly wings.

The next day, Captain Rook came to the *Pebble*.

"SURPRISE!"

Captain Rook gasped. She looked at the purple streamers. She looked at the green wobbly pudding. She looked at the flying fish drawing.

"How did you know?" she whispered. "It's all my favorite things!"

"We asked," said {{hero}}. "And we noticed."

Captain Rook hugged the drawing. Her ears wiggled.

"This is the best birthday ever," she said.

{{brother}} tried the seaweed pudding.

"Bleh," he said. But he smiled anyway.
