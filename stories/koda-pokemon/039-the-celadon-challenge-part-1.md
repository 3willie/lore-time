# The Celadon Challenge, Part One

## The Grass Gym

Celadon City was green in a way that surprised him.

Plants everywhere — window boxes, rooftop gardens, ivy climbing every wall like the city and the forest had made an agreement to share the space. It felt alive in a different way than Vermilion's industrial harbor or Cerulean's waterfront energy.

Koda liked it immediately.

He spent one night resting his team properly — good food, full recovery, quiet time together. Dratini slept curled near Arcanine's warmth, something that had become a nightly habit neither of them had made a decision about.

In the morning he went straight to the gym.

---

The Celadon Gym smelled like soil and growing things. The interior was a greenhouse — actual living walls, battle platforms elevated above garden beds, diffused light coming through a glass ceiling thick with climbing vines.

*Meadow would love this,* Koda thought.

The gym trainers were all women, all calm, all precise in the way that people who work with living things tend to be. Not aggressive. Patient.

*Patient is dangerous,* Koda reminded himself.

He worked through four gym trainers on the way to the leader — Gloom, Weepinbell, Victreebel, Exeggcute. His team rotated through cleanly. Kadabra handled the Psychic-resistant ones with careful footwork. Pidgeot used the greenhouse's unusual air currents — warm pockets rising from the garden beds — better than any opponent expected. Arcanine controlled his fire carefully, aware of the living walls around him.

Dratini handled a Tangela alone — methodical, patient, Dragon Rage cutting through the vine tangles decisively.

By the time Koda reached the far platform, his team was warm but not tired.

Erika was already there.

She sat on a low bench beside the battlefield, a book closed in her lap, unhurried. She looked up at Koda with calm dark eyes and a small polite smile.

"Three badges," she said. "You're moving carefully for someone your age."

"I'm learning," Koda said.

She stood. "Good. Then this will teach you something too."
