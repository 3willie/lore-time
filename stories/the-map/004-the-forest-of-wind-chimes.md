# The Forest of Wind Chimes

*Ting-a-ling. Ting-a-ling.*

The forest was full of wind chimes! They hung from every tree. Glass ones, wooden ones, shell ones. When the wind blew, the whole forest sang.

"It's beautiful," whispered {{brother}}.

Then they heard another sound. A little sniffle.

Under a big tree sat a tiny furry creature with long ears, like a bunny-mouse. She was holding a wind chime made of three acorns. It didn't ring at all.

"Every animal here has a chime," she sniffled. "When you hang it up, the wind blows your worries away. But mine won't ring. My worry is too big."

"What's your worry?" asked {{hero}}.

She looked down. "I'm scared of the dark," she whispered. "Everyone else is brave. If they find out, they'll laugh."

{{hero}} knelt down beside her.

Then he said something he'd never told anyone. Not even {{brother}}.

"I'm scared of the dark too," he said. "Sometimes I leave the hall light on and say it's for {{brother}}. But really, it's for me."

{{brother}}'s eyes went wide. "Really?"

"Really."

{{brother}} held his brother's hand. "That's okay," he said. "I'm scared of thunder. And bees. And the noise the bathtub makes."

The little creature's ears went up, up, up.

"You're scared," she said. "But you're still here. In a forest. At night!"

"Yep," said {{hero}}. "You can be scared *and* brave."

She stood up on her tiptoes. She hung her acorn chime on a low branch.

The wind blew.

*Clink-clonk-clink,* went her chime. Soft and happy, like a giggle.

And all the chimes in the forest rang back.

That night at home, the hall light was on. And {{hero}} didn't mind one bit.
