# The Story Game

*Pitter, patter.* It was raining. There was nothing to do.

"Let's make up a story," said Lumi. "We'll take turns. I'll start."

*Blink, blink.* "Once upon a time, there was an elephant."

"That's all?" said {{brother}}.

"Your turn. What was the elephant like?"

{{brother}} thought. "He was tiny! As small as a mouse!"

"Oh, I like that," said Lumi. "I wouldn't have thought of it. {{hero}}?"

"And he lived in a hat," said {{hero}}. "A big top hat."

"Whose hat?" asked Lumi.

"A magician's! But the magician didn't know."

"So every time the magician did a trick…" said Lumi.

"The elephant sneezed!" shouted {{brother}}. "*ACHOO!* And flowers came out of the hat!"

"And everyone clapped," said {{hero}}, "because they thought it was magic."

The boys were laughing now. The story got sillier and sillier. The elephant learned to juggle peas. He rode on a butterfly. He became the most famous tiny elephant in the world.

"The end!" said {{brother}}.

"That was a wonderful story," said Lumi. "And do you know which parts were best?"

"Which?"

"The tiny part. The hat. The sneeze. Those were all yours. I only asked the questions."

{{hero}} looked surprised. It was true.

"Can we act it out?" asked {{brother}}.

"I can't," said Lumi. "No legs. But you can."

So {{brother}} was the elephant. {{hero}} was the magician. {{dad}}'s hat was the hat.

*ACHOO!*

It rained all afternoon.

Nobody noticed.
