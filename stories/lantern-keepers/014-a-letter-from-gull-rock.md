# A Letter from Gull Rock

Every day, {{brother}} ran to the mailbox.

Every day, there were only bills and boring things.

"Nell said she would write," he sighed.

"Mail takes a long time from an island," said {{mom}}. "The boat only goes once a week."

So {{brother}} waited. Patiently. Well, *mostly* patiently.

Then, one morning, there it was. An envelope with a stamp shaped like a lighthouse. And in the corner, a tiny paw print!

"It's from Gull Rock!" shouted {{brother}}.

{{mom}} read it out loud.

*Dear Keepers,*

*The blue lamp misses you. So do the birds. Ember has been sleeping on your bunk bed every night.*

*The seal pup came back to visit with her mother. She is much bigger now.*

*Old Wick says hello. He also says somebody ate all the cookies again. (It was Ember.)*

*Love, Nell*

The boys laughed and laughed.

"We have to write back," said {{hero}}.

So they did. {{hero}} wrote the words, very carefully. {{mom}} helped with the tricky ones.

*Dear Nell and Old Wick and Ember,*

*We have pocket lanterns that glow when we think of you. They glow a lot.*

{{brother}} couldn't write many words yet. So he drew a picture. Gull Rock, with all the lamps lit up. And two boys waving. And a fox eating a cookie.

They put it in an envelope. {{brother}} licked the stamp. *Bleh!*

"Now it's Nell's turn to wait," said {{hero}}.

"I hope she's patient," said {{brother}}.

"She learned from the blue lamp too," said {{mom}}.
