# What They Built

The structure appeared through the scrubland like something that had decided the landscape didn't matter — a low sprawling compound, prefabricated walls, antenna arrays along the roof sending interference patterns into the air that Koda could feel like static against his skin.

Three guards outside. Two Pokémon patrols visible. Cages along the eastern wall — a long row, occupied.

Koda and Athena were flat in the dry grass thirty meters out, watching.

"Twelve cages minimum," Athena murmured. "All wild Pokémon. I can feel them — they're exhausted. Whatever they're doing in there has been draining them."

Koda looked at the antenna arrays.

*They're using the Pokémon as a power source,* he realized. *The interference in the air — it's being generated by forced energy output. They're taking it from them.*

His jaw tightened.

"How do you want to do this?" Athena asked.

He watched the patrol pattern. Thirty second rotation. A gap on the western wall every forty-five seconds when both patrols faced east simultaneously.

"Fast," he said. "We go through the western gap. You get to the cages. I handle what comes at us."

"And if a lot comes at us?"

He looked at her.

"Then I handle a lot."

---

They moved on the next gap.

Arcanine and Dragonair with Koda. Eevee and Pidgeotto with Athena. Through the western gap clean — no alarm, no shout.

Then a guard turned early.

"HEY—"

Everything happened at once.

Two Rocket agents released Pokémon simultaneously — Arbok and Weezing, the standard pair, moving with practiced coordination. Three more agents came around the eastern corner at a run, releasing as they moved. Electrode. Golbat. Victreebel.

Five Pokémon. Two trainers.

*Fast. Don't let them set up.*

"Athena — cages. Go. Don't stop."

"Koda—"

"Go!"

She went.

---

Arbok and Weezing came first — the coordinated pair, Arbok cutting low while Weezing floated wide to flank.

"Arcanine — Arbok. Dragonair — Weezing."

Arcanine intercepted Arbok with Extreme Speed, the collision stopping Arbok's momentum entirely. Close quarters, relentless, Arcanine fighting with the controlled aggression of a Pokémon that understood exactly how serious this was.

Dragonair swept wide and hit Weezing with Thunder Wave before it could deploy its Smokescreen — grounding it, slowing it, following immediately with Dragon Rage that sent Weezing spinning into the compound wall.

Electrode rolled fast from the right — Koda felt the electrical charge building.

*It's going to Explode.*

"Dragonair — Wrap it. Hold it away from the cages!"

Dragonair moved faster than Koda had seen it move — crossing the compound in a fluid surge, coiling around Electrode before it could reach the cage line. The explosion risk contained. Dragonair held it away from everything that mattered, the electrical discharge crawling painfully across its scales.

"Hold!" Koda called.

Dragonair held.

Golbat came screaming from above — diving fast, Supersonic aimed at Arcanine still locked with Arbok.

Koda stepped forward.

He let the air move.

Not subtle. Not careful. A hard upward column that hit Golbat mid-dive and redirected it straight up — fast and disorienting, sending it tumbling above the compound into open sky where it couldn't target anyone.

An agent shouted. Pointed at Koda.

Three agents converged on him directly.

Victreebel launched from the left — all acid and vine, fast and aggressive.

Koda felt everything at once — Dragonair straining with Electrode, Arcanine still fighting Arbok, Golbat recovering above, Victreebel closing fast, three agents between him and any exit.

*Okay.*

He let the air open up around him fully.

Wind moved through the compound in a hard organized ring — not wild, controlled, pushing the three converging agents back two steps, disrupting Victreebel's trajectory mid-lunge, creating a clear space around him.

Victreebel hit the side of the air column and skidded wide.

Koda pointed at it.

"Arcanine — finish Arbok. Then Victreebel. Now!"

Arcanine broke from Arbok with a decisive Flamethrower that ended the battle cleanly — pivoted without stopping, crossing to Victreebel before it could recover from the wind redirect, Extreme Speed into Flamethrower combination landing hard.

Victreebel went down.

Above — Golbat was still orienting.

"Dragonair — release Electrode, Dragon Rage immediately after!"

Dragonair released the contained Electrode — now too drained from the held Explosion attempt to do anything — and Dragon Rage hit it the moment it was free, the force sending it rolling harmlessly into the compound's outer wall.

Down.

Golbat dove again.

Koda looked at it.

He raised one hand and pushed a focused column of air upward — harder this time, continuous, keeping Golbat pinned above the compound in a sustained wind column it couldn't fight through no matter how hard it beat its wings.

It hung there, struggling, going nowhere.

The three agents who'd converged on him had stopped advancing.

They looked at the compound. Every Pokémon they'd sent down. At Koda standing in the center of a moving ring of air with one hand raised keeping their Golbat suspended above the roofline.

One of them reached for his radio.

"Arcanine," Koda said quietly.

Arcanine stepped forward into the agent's sightline.

The radio stayed in the agent's hand, unused.

From the eastern wall — the sound of cage doors opening. One after another. Then the particular sound of frightened wild Pokémon finding open space and taking it — scrambling, running, the desperate grateful rush of animals that had been contained too long.

Athena was working fast.

Koda kept his position. Kept the air moving. Kept Golbat pinned and the agents still.

*Thirty seconds,* he thought. *Give her thirty seconds.*

He counted the air.

---

Athena appeared at the corner of the compound.

"Done," she said. Her voice was steady but her eyes were bright — the particular look she got when animals were in distress and she could feel all of it.

Koda released the wind column.

Golbat dropped ten feet, caught itself, and immediately fled south without looking back.

He looked at the agents.

"We're leaving," he said. "You're not going to follow us."

He said it the way Arcanine stood — not as a threat. As a fact.

The agents didn't move.

---

They cleared the compound perimeter at a controlled walk — fast enough to create distance, measured enough to stay ready.

A hundred meters into the scrubland Athena stopped and crouched, hands shaking slightly now that the immediate thing was over. Eevee pressed against her immediately.

She put both hands on Eevee and breathed.

"How many?" Koda asked.

"Twenty-three," she said. "All gone now."

He looked back at the compound — still and dark against the amber sky, its antenna arrays silent for the first time.

The interference in the air was already clearing. The natural current returning, finding its old paths through the scrubland like water returning to a riverbed.

Dragonair moved beside him and rested its head against his shoulder — something it had never done standing up before.

He put a hand on it.

*I know. That was hard. You held when it mattered.*

Arcanine pressed to his other side.

All of them tired. All of them whole.

Athena stood and looked at the clearing air with something complicated on her face.

"Sabrina knew," she said.

"She knew," Koda agreed.

"And she waited for someone to go look."

"She needed someone who would handle it the right way." He looked at her. "Not just power through it."

Athena was quiet for a moment.

Then she looked at the scrubland — already, somewhere in the distance, the sound of wildlife returning. Cautious. Testing. Coming back to a place that had stopped being wrong.

"Okay," she said softly.

Just that. Okay.

They turned west toward Saffron City and walked back through the clearing air as the last of the evening light went gold and then dark around them.
