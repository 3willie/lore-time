# The Bird Feeder

"I want to build a bird feeder," said {{hero}}. "But I don't know how."

"Let's make a plan," said Lumi. "What do birds need?"

"Seeds!" said {{brother}}.

"And somewhere to stand," said {{hero}}.

"Good thinking," said Lumi. "What could hold the seeds?"

The boys looked around. A cup? A bowl? An empty milk carton!

"That could work," said Lumi. "Cut a window in the side. Put a stick through for the birds to stand on."

{{dad}} helped with the cutting. {{hero}} poked the stick through. {{brother}} poured in the seeds. They hung it in the apple tree.

Then they waited by the window.

Along came… a squirrel.

It jumped onto the feeder. It ate every single seed. *Munch, munch, munch.* Then it ran away.

"Hey!" shouted {{brother}}.

"It didn't work," said {{hero}} sadly.

"It worked for the squirrel," said Lumi. "Now you know something new. What could you change?"

{{hero}} thought. "Squirrels climb along the branch. What if we hang it on a long string, far from the branch?"

"Try it and see," said Lumi.

They tried it.

The squirrel came back. It reached. It stretched. It wobbled. It couldn't get there! It ran away with a grumpy flick of its tail.

And then — *flutter, flutter* — a little red bird landed on the stick. It pecked a seed.

"We did it!" whispered {{hero}}.

"You had the idea," said Lumi. "You built it. You fixed it."

"You helped us think," said {{brother}}.

"That's my favorite thing to do," said Lumi.

{{dad}} put a few seeds on the ground. For the squirrel.
