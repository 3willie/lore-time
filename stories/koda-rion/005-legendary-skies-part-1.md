# Legendary Skies, Part One

Koda squinted across the clifftop at his two rivals. The wind up here was something else — sharp and constant, pulling at jackets and hair, carrying the smell of storm and altitude.

Max stood with his arms folded, Poké Ball already in hand, grinning like this was the best day of his life. It probably was.

Rion stood opposite, quieter, one hand resting on the Poké Ball at his hip. He'd been quieter since the harbor. Koda didn't push it.

"Three way," Max announced. "No tag-outs. Last legendary standing."

Koda turned his ball over once in his fingers. Inside, he felt the faint electric hum even through the casing. *Ready when you are.*

"Let's go."

---

Three balls split the sky simultaneously.

**Zapdos** burst upward in a column of crackling yellow, wings snapping wide, eyes fierce and ancient. **Moltres** erupted beside it — a rolling wave of orange and red, trailing embers that floated down like lazy stars. **Articuno** unfolded last, silent and vast, ice-blue against the grey sky, each wingbeat sending frost spiraling outward in perfect rings.

For one suspended moment all three legendaries simply looked at each other across the open air.

Then the sky became chaos.

---

Moltres moved first — **Heat Wave**, broad and sweeping, aimed at both opponents at once. Max laughed as the air shimmered red.

Articuno banked hard left, ice-tipped wings cutting through the heat, and answered with **Blizzard** — a wall of white that swallowed the clifftop in frozen mist. Koda covered his face with his arm.

Zapdos didn't dodge.

It flew *into* the blizzard and detonated a **Discharge** from the inside. The explosion of lightning and ice lit up the cloudbank above them like a second sun, and all three legendaries tumbled in the shockwave before steadying themselves on enormous wings.

"Zapdos — **Thunderbolt**, focus on Moltres!"

"Moltres — **Sky Attack!**" Max's voice was gleeful. "Don't let it breathe!"

Rion said nothing. He watched both battles at once, eyes calm, waiting.

*He's reading us,* Koda realized. *He's letting us tire them out.*

"Rion, don't you dare—"

"**Sheer Cold.**"

Articuno pulled its wings in and *dropped* — then released a single concentrated beam of absolute zero from its beak. It wasn't aimed at Zapdos. It was aimed at the air between Zapdos and Moltres, forcing them apart, splitting the battlefield down the middle.

Suddenly Koda and Max were on opposite sides of an ice wall ten feet thick.

Max's distant voice: "Oh that is *dirty!*"
