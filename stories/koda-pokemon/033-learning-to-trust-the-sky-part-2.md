# Learning to Trust the Sky, Part Two

## Third Battle — The Hard One

A trainer near a small bridge had a Spearow — fast, aggressive, all beak and noise. It came in immediately, not giving Dratini time to settle.

Spearow's first Peck landed before Dratini could track it. The second hit harder. Dratini recoiled and looked back at Koda with something complicated in its expression.

Not fear. More like — *this doesn't feel worth it.*

Koda felt the pull to intervene. Felt the air in his hands, ready.

He kept it still.

"I know," he said quietly. "It's fast. You're faster than you think."

Dratini turned back. Spearow was already diving again.

This time Dratini didn't pull back — it dropped low and let Spearow pass over it, then came up underneath with a Slam that connected solidly with Spearow's chest mid-dive.

Spearow hit the ground and didn't immediately get up.

Dratini looked at its own body — surprised at what it had just done.

Koda exhaled slowly.

*It trusted itself.*

## Camp — The Quiet Part

That night Koda made camp beside a wide stream. He let all four Pokémon out — something he'd started doing every evening, giving them space to just exist together without the structure of travel or battle.

Arcanine had taken to Dratini the same way he took to most things — warmly, patiently, without making a big event of it. He'd simply started walking closer to Dratini during the days, and Dratini had stopped moving away.

Kadabra observed Dratini with quiet interest, the way it observed most things — not warmly exactly, but with genuine attention. Respectful.

Pidgeot and Dratini had developed something unexpected. They'd circle each other in the evenings — Pidgeot in the air, Dratini moving through the stream shallows below — tracking each other in lazy parallel loops. Neither close nor distant. Just aware.

*They understand each other,* Koda thought. *Different elements, same kind of movement.*

He watched Dratini glide through the shallow water, unhurried, the stream parting around it naturally.

It stopped near the bank and looked up at him.

"You're getting there," Koda said.

Dratini held his gaze a beat longer than it used to.
