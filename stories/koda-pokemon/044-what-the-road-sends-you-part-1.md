# What the Road Sends You, Part One

Two days north of Celadon the terrain changed — flatter, more open, farmland spreading wide on both sides of the road. The kind of country where you could see a long way in every direction and the sky felt bigger than usual.

Koda had been walking quietly for most of the morning when he heard it.

Not danger. Not a battle.

A sound he hadn't expected out here — laughter. Young, familiar.

He slowed.

A trainer about his age came over a low hill from the east path, moving fast, a Pokémon he didn't immediately recognize bounding alongside her. She had short dark hair, a worn travel bag, and the kind of easy confidence that came from spending real time outdoors.

She stopped when she saw him.

He stopped when he saw her.

---

"Koda."

"Athena."

---

She looked exactly like herself — curious eyes moving immediately across his team, the way they always did, reading Pokémon before people. They settled on Dragonair and stayed there.

"You caught a Dratini," she said.

"It chose me," he corrected.

She accepted that distinction with a nod. "Four badges?"

He showed her the case briefly. She had three — Pewter, Cerulean, Celadon. A different route than his, parallel paths crossing here in the middle of open farmland.

The Pokémon beside her was an Eevee — watching Koda's team with bright attentive eyes, not nervous, just deeply interested.

"It came to me in Celadon," Athena said, reading his look. "Just walked up to me outside the gym. Sat down. Looked at me like — well. Like it had already made a decision."

Koda understood that completely.

---

They walked together through the farmland without discussing it, the way he and Meadow had simply fallen into step. Some things just organized themselves.

Athena moved differently than Meadow — lighter, more lateral, always slightly angled toward whatever wildlife was nearby. She'd pause mid-sentence when a Pidgey landed in a field ahead, reading it, then continue talking like no time had passed.

Her Eevee stayed close but watched everything. Processing constantly.

"Have you run into anyone else?" Koda asked.

"Not yet." She glanced at him. "You?"

"Meadow. Outside Vermilion. We traveled together for a while."

Athena smiled — small and genuine. "Of course Meadow's here."

"She went a different direction at Vermilion City. Still out there somewhere."

"She's always fine," Athena said. Not dismissive — certain. The kind of certainty that came from knowing someone well.
