# Back to Gull Rock

The little boat went *putt-putt-putt* across the sea.

Out of the fog came a big gray rock. And a tall white lighthouse. And birds. *Squawk! Squawk!*

"GULL ROCK!" shouted {{brother}}.

It was summer again.

On the dock stood Old Wick, with his fluffy beard. And Nell, with her stick. And a red blur that ran down the dock so fast it almost fell in the sea.

"EMBER!"

Ember jumped into {{brother}}'s arms and licked his whole face.

"Welcome back, keepers," said Old Wick.

"You remembered!" said {{hero}}.

"You don't forget keepers," said Nell.

They climbed the round-and-round stairs. The green lamp. The blue lamp, with birds snoozing beside it. The silver lamp. The little red lamp, glowing on the kitchen windowsill.

At the very top, the Great Lamp shone gold.

Old Wick reached into his biggest pocket. He took out two shiny brass keys, on two blue ribbons.

"Every keeper has a key to the lighthouse," he said. "These are yours."

{{brother}}'s mouth fell open. "Real keys? For us?"

"You lit the Great Lamp," said Nell. "You found what the red lamp was for. You looked after this place, even from far away."

{{hero}} held his key very tight. It felt heavy and warm.

"So we're real keepers now?" he asked.

"You were keepers from the very first day," said Old Wick, with a wink. "You just didn't know it yet."

That night, the boys lay in their old bunk beds. Ember curled up between them.

Through the window, the Great Lamp swept across the sea. Round and round.

Home.
