# The Sky Trial

The Sky Trial was a race through the Needle Rocks — a forest of tall stone spires poking out of the clouds. Every young rider at Highcrag would fly it. The fastest would win a golden ribbon.

"Skye's going to win," said {{brother}}. "Everyone says so."

"Cinder's going to do great too," said {{hero}}. He meant it.

But {{brother}} looked worried. Cinder was the smallest, slowest dragon in the whole aerie. And the Needle Rocks were full of clouds so thick you couldn't see your own hand.

The horn blew. Twelve dragons leapt into the sky.

Skye was incredible. {{hero}} didn't yank or shout. He leaned, and asked, and Skye answered — slipping between the stone spires like a fish through reeds. Within a minute, they were far ahead of everyone. The finish flag was in sight.

Then {{hero}} heard it. Faint, from somewhere back in the clouds behind him. A hiccup. A frightened, smoky hiccup.

He looked back. The others were flying out of the clouds one by one. But not Cinder. Not {{brother}}.

{{hero}} looked at the finish flag. So close. The golden ribbon. Everyone watching.

Then he leaned gently to the left. "Skye," he said. "Can we go back?"

Skye didn't hesitate. She turned in a wide, smooth curve and dove into the clouds.

It was gray and cold and thick as soup. {{hero}} could hear the hiccups, closer now. And then he saw them: Cinder, clinging to the side of a stone spire with all four claws, too scared to move. {{brother}}, holding on tight, pale as the clouds.

"We got lost," {{brother}} said in a wobbly voice. "Cinder can't see which way to go."

"Follow us," said {{hero}}. "Stay right by Skye's tail."

Cinder let go of the rock. And together — slowly — the two dragons flew out of the clouds, side by side.

Cinder was so relieved that he hiccupped one more time. And this time, it wasn't smoke.

It was a tiny, bright, orange *flame*.

{{brother}} whooped. Cinder looked astonished. Then very, very proud.

They crossed the finish line dead last. Both of them.

A girl called Brenna won the golden ribbon. Everyone cheered for her.

But afterward, Master Oona came and found the brothers in the stables.

"The race was never the real trial," she said. "Any fast dragon can win a race. The real trial is whether a rider turns back." She looked at {{hero}}. "You did. Skye did too, without even arguing."

She paused, and a rare smile crossed her scarred face.

"The Long Flight leaves in a month. To the Ember Isles, where the wild dragons live. I'm only taking riders I trust." She nodded at them both. "Start packing."
