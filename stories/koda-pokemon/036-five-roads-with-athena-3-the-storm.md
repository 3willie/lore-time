# Five Roads With Athena 3: The Storm

It came fast — the way late season storms did in open country, building from nothing to everything in under an hour. The sky went dark gray, then greenish-gray, then the wind arrived properly.

Koda felt it before it was visible. Of course he did.

*Big system. Moving northeast. An hour, maybe less.*

They made it to a waystation shelter at the road's edge with twenty minutes to spare — a simple structure, open sides, solid roof, used by travelers exactly for moments like this.

Two other trainers were already inside — a pair, nervous, their Pokémon huddled and unsettled by the pressure change.

Koda's team settled the moment they were under the roof. Arcanine lay down. Dragonair coiled loosely. Kadabra sat. Pidgeot tucked its wings and went still.

The other trainers watched this with visible relief — something about a calm team making the space feel safer.

Athena's Eevee pressed against Arcanine immediately. Arcanine accepted this as normal at this point.

The storm hit.

Rain sideways, thunder rolling across the flat farmland with nothing to interrupt it, lightning in long jagged lines to the east.

Koda watched it from the shelter's edge.

He felt the lightning the way he always did — a particular charge in the air, the pressure differential that built before each strike. He tracked three strikes, four, reading the storm's direction and pace.

*Moving northeast. We'll be clear in forty minutes.*

Athena appeared beside him.

"You're reading it," she said. Not a question.

"Old habit."

She watched the lightning for a moment. "The Pokémon feel it differently out here. The wild ones." She paused. "They're not afraid of the storm. They're afraid of what people do when the storm makes them nervous."

Koda thought about that.

The storm moved through in thirty-eight minutes. The air afterward was the cleanest thing he'd smelled in weeks.

He looked at Athena.

"That was a good thought," he said.

She shrugged. "I have them occasionally."
