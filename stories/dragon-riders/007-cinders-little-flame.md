# Cinder's Little Flame

That night, the wind turned cold.

The baby dragons huddled in their nest. In the middle of the nest sat three speckled eggs.

"The eggs must stay warm," said Master Oona. "Their mother is out fishing. Her fire-rocks have gone out."

"Skye can warm them!" said {{hero}}.

But Skye shook her head. She was a sky dragon. She breathed wind, not fire.

Master Oona's old dragon tried. *WHOOSH!* His flame was so big that everybody jumped back.

"Too much," said Master Oona. "That would cook them!"

The babies shivered. The eggs were getting cool.

{{brother}} looked at Cinder.

Cinder looked at his feet. His flame was tiny. Everybody knew that. It was just a hiccup.

"Your flame is little," whispered {{brother}}. "But maybe little is just right."

Cinder crept up to the fire-rocks. He took a breath.

*Hic!*

A tiny orange flame. The rocks glowed, just a bit.

*Hic! Hic!*

The rocks glowed warmer. Not too hot. Not too cold. Cozy, like a blanket.

The baby dragons wiggled closer. The little blue one yawned.

All night long, Cinder stayed awake. Every time the rocks cooled down — *hic!* — he warmed them up again. {{brother}} stayed beside him and scratched his ears.

In the morning, one of the eggs went *crack*.

Out poked a tiny orange nose.

"Cinder," whispered {{brother}}. "You did that. You kept it warm."

Cinder was too sleepy to answer.

But he was smiling.
