# First Flight

The saddles were made of soft leather, with straps that buckled around a rider's legs so they couldn't fall off.

"Probably couldn't fall off," said Master Oona. "Hold on anyway."

{{brother}} went first. Cinder was small for a dragon, but he was sturdy, and he was *so* excited. He wiggled and hopped. He flapped his wings so hard he lifted off by accident before {{brother}} was even buckled in.

"Easy," said {{brother}}, patting his neck. "Easy, Cinder. Together."

Cinder took a deep breath. Then he ran across the yard, flapping hard — and lifted. Wobbly, bumpy, tilting to one side. But up. They flew one slow circle around the courtyard, a few feet off the ground, and landed in a heap of straw.

{{brother}} sat up, hair full of hay, grinning so wide his face hurt. "WE FLEW!"

Then it was {{hero}}'s turn.

Skye was different. The moment he was buckled in, she leapt — not a run, a *leap* — and two great beats of her wings took them straight up past the courtyard walls, past the towers, into the clouds.

It was the most amazing thing {{hero}} had ever felt. The wind. The speed. The whole mountain dropping away beneath them.

"Faster!" he shouted, squeezing with his knees. "Go faster!"

Skye went faster.

"Higher! Turn! Dive!"

He pulled on the reins, left and right and down, telling her what to do, over and over.

Skye stopped in midair. She hovered for a moment. Then she tipped sideways — very gently — and dropped him into the lake at the bottom of the valley.

*SPLASH.*

{{hero}} came up spluttering. Skye was circling above him, looking down. She didn't seem angry. She seemed to be waiting.

Master Oona arrived on her own old bronze dragon and hauled him out of the water by the back of his jacket.

"She dropped me!"

"She did," Master Oona agreed. "Gently. Into water. On purpose." She handed him a towel. "You weren't flying *with* her. You were flying *at* her. Yanking and shouting. Would you like someone to do that to you?"

{{hero}} sat dripping on the shore. He thought about it.

"No," he said finally.

"A rider doesn't command," said Master Oona. "A rider *asks*. And then listens to the answer."

Skye landed beside him and folded her wings. She nudged his wet hair with her nose.

"Sorry," {{hero}} told her. "Can we try again? I'll ask this time."

Skye lowered her shoulder so he could climb up.
