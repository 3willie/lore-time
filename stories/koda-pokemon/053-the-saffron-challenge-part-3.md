# The Saffron Challenge, Part Three

## Alakazam

Kadabra emerged and felt Alakazam immediately.

Not fear. Recognition. The way a younger musician feels standing next to someone who has played for thirty years — aware of the distance between them, but not broken by it.

Alakazam hadn't moved. Both spoons held level, eyes half closed, the psychic presence in the chamber steady and total.

Sabrina stood exactly as she had at the beginning. Watching Koda more than the battlefield.

"Your Kadabra is well trained," she said. "It won't be enough."

"Maybe not," Koda said. "We'll find out together."

---

Alakazam opened with Future Sight — not an immediate attack, a delayed strike placed precisely in time, building somewhere ahead of the present moment like a storm system scheduled to arrive.

*It's not just reading the present. It's setting conditions on the future.*

Koda felt the weight of it — an attack that would land regardless of what happened in the meantime, timed perfectly to hit when Kadabra was most vulnerable.

*So we have to end this before the future it planned arrives.*

"Kadabra — Psybeam, sustained pressure. Don't stop."

Kadabra pushed forward — not cautiously, continuously, Psybeam pouring out in a steady focused stream that forced Alakazam to respond rather than dictate.

Alakazam deflected with Psychic — not blocking exactly, redirecting, bending the Psybeam sideways with casual precision.

It hadn't moved its feet.

Kadabra teleported — reappeared close, trying to get inside Alakazam's preferred range.

Alakazam was already facing the new position.

It had known.

Psychic hit Kadabra hard — lifting it, holding it suspended for one long moment, then setting it down with controlled force. Not cruel. Just definitive.

Kadabra staggered. Stayed up.

*Good. Stay up.*

"Kadabra — Disable."

Kadabra's eyes locked onto Alakazam and the Disable pulse went out — targeting the Future Sight, trying to interrupt the building strike before it landed.

Alakazam's eyes opened fully for the first time.

*That surprised it.*

The Future Sight flickered — disrupted but not fully cancelled. Weakened.

"Now — everything. Psybeam."

Kadabra pulled from whatever reserves it had left and released the strongest Psybeam Koda had seen from it — focused, furious, the kind of output that came from a Pokémon that had decided this was the moment.

Alakazam raised both spoons.

The attacks met in the center of the chamber.

The collision pushed air outward in a hard ring — Koda felt it across his whole body, the pressure wave of two psychic forces meeting at full output.

For three seconds neither moved.

Then Kadabra's beam broke.

The feedback hit Kadabra and it went down — not slowly, all at once, the way things fall when they've given everything and there's simply nothing left to give.

The weakened Future Sight landed a half second later.

Unnecessary. But it landed anyway.

Koda recalled Kadabra.

He held the ball for a moment.

*You pushed further than I've ever seen you push. That was yours.*
