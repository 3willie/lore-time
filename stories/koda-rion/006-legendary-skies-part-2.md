# Legendary Skies, Part Two

Koda pressed his palm flat against the ice and thought fast. On the other side he could hear Moltres working at it — the temperature fluctuating, the wall groaning.

Above him, Zapdos and Articuno circled each other like old rivals who had danced this dance before — because they had, for a thousand years over mountaintops and open seas.

"Zapdos," Koda said quietly. Just the name. Nothing else.

Zapdos looked down at him once.

Then it stopped circling. It tucked its wings and *rocketed* straight upward — above the ice wall, above the battlefield, above the clouds entirely. The sky went dark for half a second.

When Zapdos came back down it brought the storm with it.

**Thunder** — the real thing, drawn down from the atmosphere itself — struck the clifftop in a single deafening column that shattered the ice wall, scattered Articuno sideways across the sky, and left a blackened circle in the stone.

The mist cleared.

Max was sitting down, laughing, Moltres resting its great head on the cliff edge beside him, feathers steaming.

Rion stood with his arm raised. Articuno descended slowly and landed behind him, ruffling its frost-white feathers with quiet dignity.

Zapdos circled once overhead, then landed beside Koda, crackling with residual energy, satisfied.

Nobody had technically won.

Nobody said anything about it.

---

Max broke the silence first. "Same time next month?"

Rion almost smiled. "No."

Koda watched all three legendaries settle into an uneasy, magnificent truce at the cliff's edge — electric, fire, and ice, side by side, looking out over the world they'd been born to rule.

"Maybe give it two months," he said.
