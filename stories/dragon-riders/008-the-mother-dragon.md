# The Mother Dragon

*WHOOMP. WHOOMP. WHOOMP.*

The sky went dark. Great wings covered the sun.

The mother dragon was home.

She was HUGE. Bigger than the castle gate. Her scales were red and gold, like fire. She landed with a *THUD* and looked at the strangers by her nest.

A low rumble came from her chest. *Grrrrrrr.*

"Nobody run," said Master Oona quietly. "Nobody shout."

{{hero}}'s knees were shaking. But he remembered Choosing Day. *You do not choose a dragon. Be yourself.*

He stepped forward. Slowly. And he bowed, very low.

"Hello," he said. "We kept your eggs warm. We didn't mean to scare you."

{{brother}} bowed too. So did Cinder. He bowed so low he fell on his nose.

The mother dragon sniffed the air.

She sniffed her babies. They were safe. She sniffed the eggs. They were warm. And there was the new baby, orange and wobbly, chewing on {{brother}}'s shoelace.

The rumbling stopped.

She lowered her enormous head, right down to little Cinder. And she gave him one gentle nudge.

"That means *thank you*," whispered Master Oona.

Then the mother dragon scratched at her side. Two golden scales fell to the ground, shiny as coins.

One for each rider.

The boys flew home with the scales safe in their pockets. The little blue dragon waved the hat goodbye.

"You bowed to a wild dragon," said Master Oona. "That was brave. And polite. The best riders are both."

Back at Highcrag, lanterns were going up everywhere.

"What's happening?" asked {{brother}}.

"The Festival of Lights," said Master Oona. "It starts tomorrow."
