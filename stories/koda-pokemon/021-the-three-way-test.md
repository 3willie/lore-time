# The Three-Way Test

The path to Vermilion City crossed through a wide valley — flat, open, perfect for a battle. Koda almost didn't notice the boy sitting on a boulder until he stood up and blocked the trail.

He looked about ten. Confident stance. Three Poké Balls on his belt.

"You're the kid who beat Misty," the boy said. It wasn't a question. "Name's Reid. I want a real battle. Three on three. Full team."

Koda looked at him evenly. "Full team it is."

---

Reid opened with Machop. Koda sent out Kadabra.

Machop was fast and physical — closing distance immediately, swinging hard. Kadabra sidestepped with Teleport, reappearing behind it, landing a clean Psybeam. Machop staggered but didn't go down. It lunged again.

*It's testing Kadabra's reaction speed,* Koda thought.

"Kadabra — Confusion, steady pressure. Don't overextend."

Kadabra settled in, using precise bursts rather than big attacks, wearing Machop down methodically until it couldn't stand.

Reid nodded, switching to Voltorb.

Koda called back Kadabra and sent out Pidgeot.

Voltorb rolled fast, crackling with electricity, launching Thundershock in rapid bursts. Pidgeot climbed hard, riding the updrafts — Koda felt the airflow shifting naturally and let it be. *Pidgeot already knows the sky. Trust it.*

"Pidgeot — Steel Wing, then break high!"

Pidgeot folded into a dive, wings gleaming, clipping Voltorb clean before pulling up sharply. Voltorb sparked and rolled off course.

"Again — same move!"

Second hit landed harder. Voltorb went still.

Reid's jaw tightened. He reached for his last ball.

Graveler.

Rock solid, literally. It slammed the ground as it emerged, sending a small tremor across the valley.

Koda paused. *Kadabra's drained. Pidgeot is fast but not built for this. Arcanine.*

"You're up."

Arcanine stepped forward, calm and enormous beside Graveler's bulk.

Graveler opened with Rollout, spinning fast, gaining momentum. Arcanine leapt over the first pass — but the second curved unexpectedly and caught his side. He stumbled.

*Graveler's getting faster each roll.*

"Arcanine, don't chase it — cut it off. Flamethrower, straight down in front of you!"

Arcanine planted and fired a wall of flame directly into the ground ahead. Graveler couldn't redirect in time and rolled straight through it, heat cracking across its rocky surface. It slowed. Stopped.

"Extreme Speed. Now."

Arcanine closed the gap in a single breath. The hit was decisive.

Graveler dropped.

---

Reid stood quietly for a moment, then walked over and extended his hand.

"Your team doesn't just battle," he said. "They *think.*"

Koda shook it. "They're getting there. So am I."

He recalled all three, giving each ball a brief moment before clipping them back to his belt.

Arcanine had anchored the finish. Pidgeot had found its rhythm. Kadabra had shown patience.

Three different styles. One team.

Vermilion City could wait another hour.
