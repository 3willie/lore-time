# One Sled

Mrs. Flap had a surprise. A shiny red sled!

"It goes faster than tummies," she said. "But there is only one."

"ME FIRST!" shouted all the baby penguins.

"Me first!" shouted {{brother}}.

Everybody grabbed the sled. They pulled this way. They pulled that way. *Tug, tug, tug.*

Nobody went anywhere.

"This isn't working," said {{hero}}.

"Rangers solve problems," said Bolt. "What is the problem?"

"One sled," said {{hero}}. "And lots of us."

"Could we make a line?" said Pip, in his small voice.

"A line is slow," said {{brother}}. "I'll have to wait forever."

"I can count," said Bolt. "Counting makes waiting go faster."

So they made a line. Pip went first, because it was his idea.

*Whoosh!* Down he went.

"One!" counted Bolt. "Two! Three!" The line got shorter.

While they waited, the penguins taught {{brother}} how to waddle. He was very good at it.

"Nine! Ten! Your turn, {{brother}}!"

{{brother}} sat on the sled. {{hero}} gave him a push.

*WHOOOOSH!*

It was the fastest, bumpiest, best ride ever. And do you know what? It felt even better because everybody was cheering.

At the bottom, {{brother}} pulled the sled all the way back up the hill. He gave it to the next penguin.

"Your turn," he said.

"Waiting wasn't so bad," he told {{hero}}.

"You waddled the whole time," said {{hero}}.

"That's how you wait on Snow Planet," said {{brother}}.

And he waddled to the back of the line.
