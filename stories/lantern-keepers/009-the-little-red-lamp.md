# The Little Red Lamp

Nell was staying at Gull Rock for a while. She told the best stories.

"Did you know," she said, "there is one more lamp?"

"Another one?" said {{hero}}.

"Down in the cellar. Ada and I never found out what it was for."

The boys ran down the cellar stairs. Ember the fox ran after them.

It was dusty down there. *Achoo!* There were ropes, and buckets, and a spider in its web.

And on a high shelf sat a little red lamp. It was no bigger than an apple.

{{brother}} carried it upstairs, very carefully.

"How do we turn it on?" he asked.

"Nobody knows," said Old Wick.

So they tried everything.

They sat very still, like for the blue lamp. Nothing.

{{hero}} told it something true. "I don't like peas." Nothing.

They remembered kind things, like for the silver lamp. Nothing.

{{brother}} sang it a song. He tickled it. He said "please" three times.

Nothing, nothing, nothing.

"It's broken," said {{brother}}.

"Maybe," said Nell. "Or maybe we just don't know yet."

"I don't like not knowing," said {{hero}}.

Nell smiled. "Not knowing is where every good thing starts. I didn't know how to light the blue lamp once. Neither did you."

{{hero}} thought about that. It was true.

They put the little red lamp on the kitchen windowsill, where it could see the sea.

"We'll find out," said {{brother}}. "One day."

Ember curled up beneath it. His tail glowed softly.

And the little red lamp waited.
