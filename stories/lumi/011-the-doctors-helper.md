# The Doctor's Helper

{{hero}} fell off his bike. *Crash!*

His wrist hurt. It hurt a lot.

"Let's have Dr. Patel take a look," said {{mom}}.

At the doctor's office, Dr. Patel was gentle. "We'll take a special picture," she said. "It shows the bones inside your arm. It doesn't hurt one bit."

*Click!*

The picture came up on a screen. {{hero}} could see his own bones! They were white, like a little skeleton hand.

"Cool," whispered {{brother}}.

Then something on the screen went *blink, blink*. A small yellow circle appeared around one spot.

"Is that Lumi?" asked {{brother}}.

Dr. Patel smiled. "It's a helper, a bit like your Lumi. It has looked at thousands and thousands of bone pictures. It's very good at spotting tiny things. See? It's showing me where to look."

She leaned in close and looked for a long time.

"There," she said. "A very small crack. So small I might have missed it."

"So the helper fixed it?" asked {{hero}}.

"No," said Dr. Patel. "The helper *looks*. I *decide*. And your body does the fixing, all by itself."

She wrapped his wrist in a bright blue cast.

"Four weeks," she said. "No bikes. Lots of rest. And everybody gets to sign it."

At home, {{brother}} signed the cast first, with a big wobbly star.

{{hero}} held it up to Lumi. "The doctor has a helper like you."

"I'm glad," said Lumi. "Helpers and people make a good team."

"You can't sign my cast, though," said {{hero}}.

"No hands," said Lumi.

So {{brother}} drew a little glowing circle on it. For Lumi.
