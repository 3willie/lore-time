# Juno's Old Hat

One day, the boys found an old photo in Juno's cabin.

It showed a little girl in a big, floppy hat. She was standing next to a baby dinosaur. A very small, long-necked one.

"Who's that?" asked {{brother}}.

Juno smiled. "That's me. When I was about your age."

"YOU?" said both boys at once.

"Me," said Juno. "And that baby dinosaur is Tallulah."

The boys looked at the photo. Then out the window, at Tallulah, who was now as big as a house.

"Tallulah was *small*?" said {{brother}}.

"Everyone starts small," said Juno.

She sat down and told them the story.

When she was a little girl, she went camping with her grandpa. She found a footprint by the river. She followed it behind the waterfall.

"Just like us!" said {{hero}}.

"Just like you," said Juno. "And there was an old keeper here. His name was Ranger Bill. He was kind and very grumpy. He said, *Can you keep a secret?*"

"And you said yes!"

"I did. And every summer I came back to help. I fed the babies. I scrubbed the frills. I made lots of mistakes." She laughed. "Once, I let all the Zippers into the garden by accident."

"Did Ranger Bill get mad?" asked {{brother}}.

"A little," said Juno. "But then he taught me how to fix it. When he got too old, he gave me his hat."

She took the big, floppy hat off the hook and looked at it.

"One day," she said, "I'll be too old. And somebody will need to wear this hat."

She looked at the boys.

And she winked.
