# Learning to Trust the Sky, Part Three

## Fourth Battle — Something New

The following afternoon brought a trainer with a Slowpoke — patient, heavy, almost impossible to pressure.

Dratini tried Wrap twice. Slowpoke barely noticed.

Thunder Wave landed but Slowpoke was so naturally slow the paralysis barely changed anything. Dratini circled, frustrated, looking for an angle.

Koda watched without speaking.

*Figure it out. You can.*

Dratini shifted — something changing in its approach. It stopped trying to overpower and started moving differently. Looser. More lateral. Probing instead of committing.

Then it reared back and unleashed a Dragon Rage — not something Koda had seen from it before. A focused burst of deep blue-white energy that hit Slowpoke with enough force to end the battle cleanly.

The trainer stared. "Did — was that Dragon Rage?"

Koda stood very still for a moment.

*When did it learn that?*

He looked at Dratini. Dratini looked back with an expression that was almost — pleased with itself.

Koda laughed. Quiet and genuine.

"You've been holding out on me."

Dratini turned away like it hadn't done anything unusual and started moving down the trail.

Arcanine looked at Koda.

Koda shook his head and followed.

---

By the end of the week, Dratini moved differently than it had at the river. Not harder or faster — just more present. More willing.

It still kept something held back. Something private. The way all Dragon Pokémon seemed to carry a deeper self that they only revealed slowly, on their own terms.

Koda respected that completely.

*We're not done learning each other,* he thought. *Not even close.*

He checked the map. The next city was still two days out.

He put it away and kept walking.

Four Pokémon. All of them growing.

All of them his.
