# Lumi Can't Hug

*Zoom!* {{brother}} was running through the house.

*Bump! Thud!*

He tripped on the rug and landed on his knee.

"OWWW!"

It was a big scrape. {{brother}} began to cry.

"Oh dear," said Lumi from the kitchen table. Its light went *blink, blink*. "I heard a bump. Are you hurt?"

"My knee!" cried {{brother}}.

"I can help," said Lumi. "First, wash it gently with water. Then pat it dry. Then put on a bandage."

Those were good ideas. But {{brother}} kept crying.

"Lumi," sniffed {{brother}}. "Can you give me a hug?"

*Blink, blink, blink.*

"No," said Lumi softly. "I can't. I don't have any arms. I wish I could help with that part."

Then {{mom}} came in. She scooped {{brother}} up onto her lap and wrapped her arms all the way around him.

{{brother}} stopped crying. Just like that.

{{mom}} washed the knee. She patted it dry. She put on a bandage with a dinosaur on it.

"Lumi told us what to do," said {{hero}}.

"That was helpful, Lumi," said {{mom}}. "Thank you."

"You're welcome," said Lumi. "But I think the hug helped the most."

{{brother}} snuggled closer.

"Lumi," he said, "do *you* ever need a hug?"

*Blink, blink.* "I don't think so," said Lumi. "I'm a different kind of thing than you. But I'm glad you asked. That was kind."

{{hero}} patted the top of Lumi anyway. Gently. *Pat, pat.*

"Hugs are a people job," said {{mom}}.

"The best job," said {{brother}}.
